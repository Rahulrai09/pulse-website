OT Table Price in India: Manual, Hydraulic, Electric and C-Arm

Operation theatre tables in India range from about Rs 30,000 to Rs 7 lakh. This guide explains the price bands, which table suits which surgery, and what to check before you buy.

How much does an OT table cost in India? Bajaj Finserv's April 2026 guide puts manual OT tables at about Rs 30,000 to Rs 70,000, hydraulic tables at Rs 70,000 to Rs 1.5 lakh, electric tables at Rs 1.5 lakh to Rs 4.5 lakh, C-arm compatible tables at Rs 2 lakh to Rs 6 lakh, and orthopaedic tables at Rs 2.5 lakh to Rs 7 lakh.

Manual, Hydraulic or Electric?

  • Manual: positions are set with cranks and levers. Lowest cost, but slower to adjust during surgery.
  • Hydraulic: a foot pump raises and lowers the table, with levers for tilts. The most common choice in Indian hospitals, with no dependence on power.
  • Electric: a remote or panel controls height, tilt and sections. Faster, smoother and easier on staff, but needs power backup and service for motors.

What Drives the Price

1. Positioning range

Trendelenburg, reverse Trendelenburg, lateral tilt, back and leg sections, top slide and kidney bridge all add cost, and decide which surgeries the table can support.

2. Radiolucent top and C-arm access

Tables used with intraoperative X-ray need a radiolucent top and an eccentric column or slide so the C-arm can pass underneath.

3. Build quality

Stainless steel base and covers resist corrosion from daily cleaning. Floor locking and a stable column matter for heavier patients.

4. Accessories

Arm boards, leg holders, shoulder supports, head rests and orthopaedic traction attachments are often priced separately.

5. Service

Hydraulic seals and electric actuators need periodic service. Check spare part and engineer availability in your city.

OT Table Price in India: FAQs

How much does an OT table cost in India?

Bajaj Finserv's April 2026 guide puts manual OT tables at about Rs 30,000 to Rs 70,000, hydraulic tables at Rs 70,000 to Rs 1.5 lakh, electric tables at Rs 1.5 lakh to Rs 4.5 lakh, C-arm compatible tables at Rs 2 lakh to Rs 6 lakh and orthopaedic tables at Rs 2.5 lakh to Rs 7 lakh.

Hydraulic or electric OT table: which is better?

Hydraulic tables are reliable, need no power and cost less, so they suit most general OTs. Electric tables are faster and smoother to position and easier on staff, which suits busy multi-speciality OTs with good power backup and service support.

What is a C-arm compatible OT table?

A table with a radiolucent (X-ray transparent) top and an eccentric column or top slide, so a C-arm X-ray machine can image the patient during surgery without the base getting in the way.

What should I check before buying an OT table?

Check the positions and tilt ranges, height range, load capacity, radiolucent top and C-arm access, stainless steel build, floor locking, included accessories, and local service for hydraulics or actuators.

Are accessories included in the OT table price?

Often only basic accessories are included. Arm boards, leg holders, head rests and orthopaedic traction attachments may be extra, so ask for an itemised quote.
