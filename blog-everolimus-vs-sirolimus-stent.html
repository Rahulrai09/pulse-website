Everolimus vs Sirolimus Stent: What the Evidence Shows

Most drug-coated stents in India release one of two drugs: everolimus or sirolimus. This guide explains how they differ, what the trials show, and why the stent design and the doctor's choice matter as much as the drug.

What the Drugs Do

After a stent is put in, tissue can grow back inside it and narrow the artery again. This is called restenosis. Drug-coated stents release a drug slowly into the artery wall to stop this. Sirolimus was the first drug used this way. Everolimus is a close cousin of sirolimus and works in a similar way.

What the Trials Show

A 2012 study pooled 8 trials with 11,167 patients, followed for 9 to 36 months. It compared everolimus stents with an older sirolimus stent.

OutcomeEverolimus vs older sirolimus stent
Major heart eventsNo clear difference
Repeat treatment of the same spotNo clear difference
Definite stent clotting (thrombosis)About half the risk with everolimus
Definite or probable clottingNo clear difference

The authors concluded that the two were comparable overall, with everolimus possibly linked to less definite stent clotting.

Why the Drug Is Not the Whole Story

Those trials used a first-generation sirolimus stent. Many newer sirolimus stents have thinner struts and coatings that dissolve, so the old results may not apply to them. Other parts of the stent matter too:

  • Strut thickness: thinner struts sit closer to the artery wall.
  • Polymer: the coating that holds the drug may stay permanently or dissolve over weeks.
  • Sizes: the right diameter and length for each narrowing.
  • Deliverability: how well the stent crosses bends and grips the balloon.

We suggest hospitals stock both types in a full range of sizes, so the cardiologist can choose for each artery. In small arteries, a drug-coated balloon may be an option instead; see our drug-coated balloon vs stent guide.

Does It Change the Price?

No. In India, drug-eluting stents of both types fall under the same government price cap, so the choice is about design and fit, not cost. See our angioplasty cost guide for the full bill.

Everolimus vs Sirolimus Stent: FAQs

Which is better, an everolimus or a sirolimus stent?

Pooled trials found similar overall results, with less definite stent clotting for everolimus versus an older sirolimus stent. Newer stents of both types differ in design, so the cardiologist chooses for each artery.

What is the difference between everolimus and sirolimus?

They are closely related drugs. Both slow tissue regrowth inside the stent to stop the artery narrowing again.

Do everolimus stents cost more than sirolimus stents?

No. Drug-eluting stents of both types fall under the same government price cap in India.

What else matters besides the drug?

Strut thickness, the polymer coating, the size range and how easily the stent reaches the narrowing.

Can the patient choose the stent drug?

Patients can ask, but the cardiologist decides based on the artery and the stents available.
