Equipment by OT Zone

An OT is a set of rooms, not one room. The 2022 Indian Public Health Standards (IPHS) split it into four zones: protective, clean, sterile and dirty. Here is what each area needs.

ZoneEquipment
Pre-op and holdingStretcher trolleys, patient monitor or pulse oximeter, oxygen and suction points
Scrub areaSurgeon’s scrub sink with elbow or knee taps (a pair per OT)
Operating roomOT table, OT light, anaesthesia machine, monitor, cautery unit, suction, pumps, instrument and Mayo trolleys, IV stand, kick bucket, X-ray viewer
RecoveryRecovery beds or trolleys, a monitor per bed, oxygen and suction, crash cart with defibrillator
Sterile supply (TSSU or CSSD)Autoclave, instrument washer, packing table, sterile store racks

IPHS also asks for a sterile supply room (TSSU) next to the OT. Clean tools then do not travel far.

Room Rules Every OT Must Meet

  • Temperature: 21 ± 3 °C (IPHS 2022; NABH asks for 18 ± 2 °C for joint replacement).
  • Humidity: 50 to 60% relative humidity (NABH allows 20 to 60%).
  • Air changes: at least 20 an hour, four of them fresh air, through HEPA filters.
  • Gases: piped oxygen, nitrous oxide, medical air and suction, with a backup cylinder.
  • Power: generator and UPS backup for the light, anaesthesia machine and monitors.
  • Doors: a self-closing door with a viewing window, and a separate trolley change area.

Safety Checks Before Every Surgery

Good kit only helps if it is checked. Many Indian hospitals use the WHO Surgical Safety Checklist. Before the patient is put to sleep, the team confirms that:

  • the anaesthesia machine and medicine check is complete;
  • the pulse oximeter is on the patient and working;
  • any equipment problems are raised at the time-out and at sign-out.

Check the anaesthesia machine, defibrillator and suction every day, and log it. Keep a service contract for each big item.

How to Plan and Save

  • Match the OT table to the work. Hydraulic suits general cases. Electric suits long cases. Pick a C-arm table for bone and urology work.
  • For major cases, buy one good workstation, not two basic machines.
  • Pick monitors with EtCO2. It measures carbon dioxide in each breath.
  • Plan gas and power points before the building work starts.
  • Ask for set-up, training and two years of service in each quote.

Operation Theatre Equipment List: FAQs

What equipment is needed in an operation theatre?

A general OT needs an OT table, OT light, anaesthesia machine, monitor and defibrillator. It also needs a cautery unit, suction, pumps, trolleys, a crash cart and piped gases. Add an autoclave and surgical instrument sets.

How much does operation theatre equipment cost in India?

The main equipment for one general OT costs about Rs 10 lakh to Rs 38 lakh, based on 2026 Indian price guides. The anaesthesia machine is usually the biggest item. Super-speciality OTs with pendants and premium workstations can need Rs 40 lakh to Rs 80 lakh of equipment.

What are the four zones of an operation theatre?

IPHS 2022 splits the OT into four zones. The protective zone is for entry and changing. The clean zone holds pre-op and recovery. The sterile zone is the operating room and scrub area. The dirty zone handles used linen and waste.

What temperature and air changes does an OT need?

Keep the OT at 21 ± 3 °C and 50 to 60% humidity (IPHS 2022). The air should change at least 20 times an hour, with four of those changes fresh air. NABH also asks for HEPA-filtered air.

Which is the most important safety equipment in an OT?

The pulse oximeter and a checked anaesthesia machine come first. The WHO Surgical Safety Checklist asks the team to confirm both before every anaesthetic. A working defibrillator, suction and an oxygen backup cylinder are also essential.
