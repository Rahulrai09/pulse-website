What to Check Before Buying an OT Light

  • Single or double dome. Major theatres usually use a double dome, so the second head can light the field when the surgeon's head blocks the first.
  • Light intensity and field size. Compare lux and how far the light field can be adjusted.
  • Shadows and heat. LED lights run cooler than old halogen lights. Ask how the light handles shadows.
  • Mounting. Ceiling lights need the right ceiling height and support. Share your ceiling details at quote stage.
  • Power backup. Ask about battery backup if your area has power cuts.
  • LED life and service. Long LED life cuts running costs; confirm warranty and spares.
