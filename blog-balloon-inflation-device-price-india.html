What Does a Balloon Inflation Device Do?

During angioplasty, the doctor inflates a balloon inside the narrowed artery and then expands the stent. The inflation device is a syringe with a pressure gauge and a screw or lock, so the balloon is filled to an exact pressure in atmospheres (atm) and held there. Accurate pressure matters because each balloon and stent has a rated pressure.

What Changes the Price of an Inflation Device?

  • Maximum pressure. 30 to 40 atm devices cost more than 20 atm devices.
  • Gauge. Analog gauges are standard; digital displays cost more.
  • Handle. Gun, plunger and hybrid handles suit different hands and speeds.
  • Kit contents. Haemostasis valve, guidewire insertion tool, torque device and stopcock add value.

What to Check Before Buying Inflation Devices

  • Maximum pressure (atm) and gauge accuracy
  • Barrel volume (commonly 20 ml)
  • Lock and quick-release for fast deflation
  • Kit contents: Y-connector or haemostasis valve, insertion tool, torque device
  • Sterile packing, lot number and expiry date
  • Steady supply and bulk price

Inflation Device Price in India: FAQs

How much does a balloon inflation device cost in India?

Most 20 to 30 atm PTCA inflation devices list at about Rs 1,500 to Rs 3,000, and high-pressure (40 atm) or branded devices at about Rs 4,500 to Rs 6,000 (IndiaMART, October 2026).

What is a PTCA inflation device?

A syringe with a pressure gauge and lock used in angioplasty to fill the balloon to an exact pressure, measured in atmospheres (atm), and hold it there.

What pressure does a balloon inflation device need?

Most devices go up to 25 or 30 atm, which covers routine angioplasty and stent expansion. High-pressure devices reach 40 atm for hard lesions. Always stay within the balloon's rated pressure.

What is included in an inflation device kit?

Kits usually add a haemostasis valve (Y-connector), a guidewire insertion tool and a torque device to the inflation device.

Are inflation devices single-use?

Yes. PTCA inflation devices and kits are sterile, single-use items.
