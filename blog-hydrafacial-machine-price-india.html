Hydrafacial Machine Price in India: A Clinic Buyer's Guide

Hydrafacial (hydradermabrasion) machines in India range from under Rs 2 lakh to about Rs 10 lakh. This guide explains the price bands, what extra handpieces add, and the serum costs that decide profitability.

How much does a hydrafacial machine cost in India? Bajaj Finserv's August 2026 guide puts portable machines at Rs 1.5 lakh to Rs 3 lakh, standard machines at Rs 2 lakh to Rs 4 lakh, advanced machines at Rs 4 lakh to Rs 7 lakh, and multi-functional machines at Rs 5 lakh to Rs 9 lakh. A May 2026 clinic guide puts medical-grade systems at Rs 3 lakh to Rs 10 lakh.

What a Hydrafacial Machine Does

A hydrafacial (hydradermabrasion) treatment combines three steps through one vacuum handpiece: cleansing and exfoliation, extraction of blocked pores, and infusion of serums. Multi-functional machines add handpieces such as ultrasound, radiofrequency, oxygen spray, cold hammer or LED masks, so one device can offer several facial treatments.

What Drives the Price

1. Number of handpieces

Basic machines have a hydra-dermabrasion handpiece only. Multi-functional platforms add ultrasound, RF and other tips, which raises the price but lets the clinic offer more services.

2. Vacuum control and serum delivery

Stable, adjustable suction and a reliable serum bottle system matter for consistent results and comfort.

3. Build and certification

Medical-grade machines with proper certification and quality pumps last longer and are safer than consumer-grade units.

4. Serums and tips (running costs)

Serums and disposable tips are the main ongoing costs. Ask whether the machine works with your choice of serums or only with branded refills.

5. Training and service

Staff training and fast pump and handpiece repair keep the machine earning.

Hydrafacial Machine Price in India: FAQs

How much does a hydrafacial machine cost in India?

Bajaj Finserv's August 2026 guide puts portable hydrafacial machines at about Rs 1.5 lakh to Rs 3 lakh, standard machines at Rs 2 lakh to Rs 4 lakh, advanced machines at Rs 4 lakh to Rs 7 lakh and multi-functional machines at Rs 5 lakh to Rs 9 lakh.

What is the difference between a hydrafacial and a regular facial machine?

A hydrafacial machine uses a vacuum handpiece to cleanse, exfoliate, extract and infuse serums in one pass, which gives more consistent results than manual facials. Multi-functional machines add ultrasound, RF and other handpieces.

What are the running costs of a hydrafacial machine?

Mainly serums and disposable tips for each session, plus filters, maintenance of the vacuum pump and an AMC. Check whether the machine accepts serums from different suppliers.

Is a portable hydrafacial machine good enough for a clinic?

Portable machines suit salons and low volumes. Clinics doing several treatments a day usually choose a standard or multi-functional machine with stronger, steadier suction and more handpieces.

What should a clinic check before buying?

Check the handpieces included, suction control, serum system, certification, warranty on pumps and handpieces, training, serum costs and local service.
