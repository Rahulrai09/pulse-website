Picosecond Laser Machine Price in India: Pico vs Q-Switched Nd:YAG

Picosecond lasers are among the most expensive machines in an aesthetic clinic. This guide explains the price range, how pico lasers compare with Q-switched Nd:YAG for tattoos and pigmentation, and what to check before you invest.

How much does a picosecond laser cost in India? A May 2026 clinic setup guide by dermatologist Dr. Chirranjiv Chabraa puts picosecond laser machines at about Rs 25 lakh to Rs 60 lakh, and dedicated Q-switched Nd:YAG tattoo removal machines at about Rs 4 lakh to Rs 12 lakh. Wavelengths, pulse duration, energy and beam quality decide the price.

Picosecond vs Q-Switched: What Is the Difference?

Both shatter pigment with very short laser pulses. Q-switched lasers fire in nanoseconds (billionths of a second). Picosecond lasers fire in picoseconds (trillionths of a second), so they break pigment into finer particles with more of a pressure (photoacoustic) effect and less heat. In practice, pico lasers are often used for stubborn tattoos, pigmentation such as melasma and skin rejuvenation, while Q-switched lasers remain a cost-effective workhorse.

What Drives the Price

1. Pulse duration and energy

Shorter pulses with higher peak power cost more to engineer and are the main reason pico lasers are expensive.

2. Wavelengths

1064 nm treats dark ink and deeper pigment; 532 nm treats red and orange ink and superficial pigment. Extra wavelengths add cost.

3. Beam profile and handpieces

A flat-top (top-hat) beam spreads energy evenly. Fractional and zoom handpieces widen the range of treatments.

4. Repetition rate and speed

Higher repetition rates speed up large tattoos and full-face treatments.

5. Maintenance and service

Laser rods, flash lamps and articulated arms need specialised service. Check the warranty and the cost of replacing parts.

Picosecond Laser Machine Price in India: FAQs

How much does a picosecond laser cost in India?

A May 2026 clinic setup guide by dermatologist Dr. Chirranjiv Chabraa puts picosecond laser machines at about Rs 25 lakh to Rs 60 lakh, and dedicated Q-switched Nd:YAG tattoo removal machines at about Rs 4 lakh to Rs 12 lakh.

Is a picosecond laser better than a Q-switched laser?

Picosecond lasers use much shorter pulses, which break pigment into finer particles with less heat, and are often preferred for stubborn tattoos and pigmentation. Q-switched lasers cost far less and remain effective for many tattoo and pigment treatments.

What can a picosecond laser treat?

Tattoo removal, pigmentation such as freckles and sun spots, and, with fractional handpieces, skin rejuvenation, pores and acne scars. Treatment choice is made by the treating dermatologist.

Which wavelength removes which tattoo colour?

1064 nm targets black and dark blue ink and deeper pigment. 532 nm targets red, orange and some other warm colours. Other colours may need additional wavelengths.

What should a clinic check before buying a pico laser?

Check pulse duration, peak energy, wavelengths, beam profile, handpieces, repetition rate, certification, warranty on the laser source, service response and training.
