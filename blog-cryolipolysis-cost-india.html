Cryolipolysis Cost in India: Fat Freezing Price by Body Area

Cryolipolysis, or fat freezing, reduces stubborn fat without surgery. CoolSculpting is one well-known brand. This guide shows what it costs in India by area, why prices vary, and how many sessions people need.

Cryolipolysis (fat freezing) in India costs about Rs 20,000 to Rs 60,000 a session depending on the body area, according to a health platform guide updated in July 2026. Branded CoolSculpting applicators are listed at Rs 40,000 to Rs 65,000 each in one Bengaluru clinic, while some clinics list cryolipolysis from about Rs 5,000. Most need 1 to 3 sessions.

How Many Sessions?

Most people need 1 to 3 sessions per area for a visible change, depending on the amount of fat. A published review found average fat-layer reductions of roughly 10% to 28% after treatment. Results build over the next few weeks as the body clears the frozen fat cells. Treated fat cells do not come back. But if you gain weight, fat can build up elsewhere, so diet and exercise still matter.

Who Is It For?

  • People close to their goal weight who have small pockets of fat, such as a double chin, belly or love handles.
  • It is not a weight-loss treatment and does not tighten loose skin.
  • Redness, numbness and soreness in the treated area are common for a short time. A doctor should check you first.

Does Insurance Cover Cryolipolysis?

Usually not. It is treated as a cosmetic procedure. Many clinics offer packages or payment plans.

For Clinics: What a Session Has to Pay For

Most clinic cryolipolysis machines list at about Rs 1.5 lakh to Rs 3.5 lakh in India, and premium 360-degree systems at about Rs 5 lakh to Rs 15 lakh (see our cryolipolysis machine price guide). As a rough example (our estimate), a Rs 6 lakh machine needs about 30 sessions at Rs 20,000 to cover its price, before cooling membranes, staff and rent.
