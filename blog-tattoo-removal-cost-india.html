Laser Tattoo Removal Cost in India: Per Session and Full Course

Laser tattoo removal is priced per session, and most tattoos need several. This guide shows what clinics in India charge by tattoo size, how many sessions to expect, which inks take longer and how picosecond and Q-switched lasers compare.

Laser tattoo removal in India costs about Rs 1,500 to Rs 20,000 per session, depending on tattoo size and city. A Mumbai skin clinic charges Rs 3,000 to Rs 10,000 for a small tattoo and Rs 6,000 to Rs 20,000 or more for a large one. Most tattoos need several sessions, usually 6 to 8 weeks apart.

Tattoo Removal Cost by Tattoo Size

Tattoo sizeCost per session
Small (up to 4 square inches)Rs 3,000 to Rs 10,000
Medium (up to 12 square inches)Rs 5,000 to Rs 15,000
Large (more than 12 square inches)Rs 6,000 to Rs 20,000 or more

These are a Mumbai skin clinic's prices, updated in November 2026. A Lucknow clinic lists Rs 1,500 to Rs 4,000 per session (2023). Big-city clinics and newer lasers usually charge more.

How Many Sessions Are Needed?

  • A Lucknow clinic says professional tattoos usually take about 5 to 8 sessions, and home-made tattoos about 4 to 6.
  • In a large study of Q-switched laser treatment, 47% of tattoos were cleared after 10 sessions and 75% after 15.
  • Sessions are usually spaced 6 to 8 weeks apart, so full removal can take a year or more.

Doctors advise against promising an exact number of sessions, because every tattoo is different.

What Makes Removal Slower

  • Ink colour: black and blue are easiest. Red, green and multi-colour tattoos need more sessions.
  • Size: tattoos larger than 30 cm² respond more slowly.
  • Place on the body: tattoos on the feet or legs clear more slowly.
  • Age and density: tattoos older than 3 years and dense colour take longer.
  • Smoking is linked to slower clearing.

These factors come from the same large study, as reported by a US dermatology news service.

A US laser expert finds picosecond lasers generally more effective than older Q-switched lasers, and notes they cost more. For clinics, our picosecond laser price guide puts pico machines at about Rs 25 lakh to Rs 60 lakh and Q-switched machines at about Rs 4 lakh to Rs 12 lakh.

Side Effects

Common side effects are redness, blisters, lighter or darker skin patches and, less often, scars. Cosmetic tattoos can sometimes turn darker after laser. Go to a dermatologist, follow aftercare and keep the area out of the sun.
