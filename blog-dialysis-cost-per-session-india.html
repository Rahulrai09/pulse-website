Dialysis Cost per Session in India (2026): Private and Government

Most people on dialysis need 2 or 3 sessions a week, so the cost adds up fast. Here is what one session costs, what a month and a year cost, and what schemes pay.

PBy Pulse Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 5 min read
Share

One haemodialysis session in India usually costs about Rs 1,000 to Rs 5,000 at private hospitals and dialysis centres. With 3 sessions a week, that is about Rs 24,000 to Rs 60,000 a month. Dialysis is free for BPL patients at district hospitals under the Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme.

Dialysis Cost per Session at Private Centres

Published price ranges from Indian hospital groups and dialysis chains, checked in November 2026:

SourcePrice per haemodialysis session
NephroPlus (dialysis chain), October 2025About Rs 1,000 – 4,000
Medicover HospitalsRs 2,000 – 5,000
Yashoda Hospitals, conventional haemodialysisRs 1,500 – 4,000

The fee depends on the type of centre, the type of dialysis and the city.

NephroPlus splits the cost per session by type of centre:

Type of centrePrice per session
Government-aided centresAbout Rs 500 – 1,500

Free dialysis for BPL patients is covered further down.

Cost by Type of Dialysis

Yashoda Hospitals lists these ranges per session for different kinds of haemodialysis:

TypePrice per session
Conventional haemodialysisRs 1,500 – 4,000
High-flux haemodialysisRs 3,000 – 7,000
Haemodiafiltration (HDF)Rs 5,000 – 12,000
SLED (slow dialysis in the ICU)Rs 8,000 – 25,000

SLED is slow dialysis for very sick patients in the ICU. That is why it costs much more.

Monthly and Yearly Cost of Dialysis

Most patients need dialysis 2 or 3 times a week, as advised by their doctor. Medicover gives these figures:

TypeCost
Haemodialysis, 3 sessions a weekRs 24,000 – 60,000 a month
Home haemodialysisRs 30,000 – 80,000 a month
Peritoneal dialysisRs 20,000 – 40,000 a month

Over 12 months, Rs 24,000 to Rs 60,000 a month works out to about Rs 2.9 to 7.2 lakh a year for centre haemodialysis. Our dialysis sessions per week guide explains why the number of sessions matters.

Costs on Top of the Session Fee

We suggest budgeting for these as well:

  • Medicines and injections your nephrologist prescribes.
  • Regular blood tests to check that dialysis is working.
  • Vascular access: an AV fistula or a catheter. See AV fistula vs catheter.
  • Travel to the centre two or three times a week, often with a family member.
  • Lost work time for the patient and carer.

Free Dialysis: PMNDP

The Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme (PMNDP) started on 7 April 2016 under the National Health Mission. It gives free dialysis to the poor, with two parts: haemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis (PMNDP portal).

A registered patient can also take a free slot at any dialysis centre in the country, if one is available. To find a centre, ask at your district hospital or check the PMNDP portal. We suggest carrying your BPL card, Aadhaar and your nephrologist’s prescription.

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

PM-JAY has haemodialysis packages at empanelled hospitals, so approved sessions are cashless for eligible families. In April 2026 the National Health Authority changed the rules. One approval now covers up to six sessions. Each session needs a fingerprint check at entry and exit, and can last up to four hours (Department of Information and Public Relations, Nagaland).

We suggest checking with the hospital’s Ayushman desk before your first session.

What CGHS Pays per Session

CGHS changed its rates on 13 October 2025. Here are the rates for a semi-private ward in a Tier II city:

ProcedureNon-NABHNABH
Haemodialysis, sero-negativeRs 1,913Rs 2,250
Haemodialysis, sero-positiveRs 2,295Rs 2,700
Peritoneal dialysisRs 2,754Rs 3,240
SLEDRs 7,038Rs 8,280

"Sero-positive" means the patient has hepatitis B, hepatitis C or HIV. The same CGHS order says Tier II rates are 10% below Tier I, and Tier III rates are 20% below. Check the list for your city.

How to Lower the Cost

  • Check whether you qualify for free dialysis under PMNDP or a state scheme.
  • Check your PM-JAY, CGHS, ESI or private insurance cover before you start.
  • Compare dialysis centres near you.
  • We suggest asking each centre what the fee includes: dialyser, blood lines, injections and tests.
  • Ask whether the dialyser is single-use. See our single-use device rules guide.

Questions to Ask a Dialysis Centre

  • What is the fee per session, and what does it include?
  • Is a nephrologist on site or on call during sessions?
  • How are the machines and water treatment checked?
  • Can I get a fixed slot that suits my work?
  • Do you accept PM-JAY, CGHS or my insurance?

Dialysis Cost in India: Key Facts

  • One haemodialysis session costs about Rs 1,000 to Rs 5,000 at private centres in India.
  • With 3 sessions a week, the monthly cost is about Rs 24,000 to Rs 60,000 (Medicover), or about Rs 2.9 to 7.2 lakh a year.
  • Dialysis is free for the poor at district hospitals under PMNDP, started on 7 April 2016.
  • CGHS pays Rs 1,913 to Rs 2,250 per haemodialysis session in Tier II cities (rates from 13 October 2025).
  • From April 2026, PM-JAY allows up to six dialysis sessions per approval, each up to four hours.

Dialysis Cost in India: FAQs

What is the cost of one dialysis session in India?

About Rs 1,000 to Rs 5,000 per haemodialysis session at private hospitals and dialysis centres. NephroPlus puts it at about Rs 1,000 to Rs 4,000, and Medicover at Rs 2,000 to Rs 5,000.

How much does dialysis cost per month in India?

With 3 sessions a week, about Rs 24,000 to Rs 60,000 a month at private centres, according to Medicover. Medicines, tests and travel cost extra.

How much does dialysis cost per year in India?

At Rs 24,000 to Rs 60,000 a month, centre haemodialysis works out to about Rs 2.9 to 7.2 lakh a year, before medicines, tests and travel.

What is the cost of peritoneal dialysis in India?

Medicover puts peritoneal dialysis at about Rs 20,000 to Rs 40,000 a month. CGHS pays Rs 2,754 to Rs 3,240 per peritoneal dialysis session in Tier II cities.

Is dialysis free in government hospitals?

Yes for the poor. Under the Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme, started in 2016, dialysis is free for BPL patients at district hospitals. NephroPlus puts other government-aided centres at about Rs 500 to Rs 1,500 per session.

Does Ayushman Bharat cover dialysis?

Yes. PM-JAY has haemodialysis packages at empanelled hospitals, so approved sessions are cashless. From April 2026, one approval covers up to six sessions, with a fingerprint check at entry and exit.

What does CGHS pay for dialysis?

For a Tier II city, CGHS pays Rs 1,913 (non-NABH) or Rs 2,250 (NABH) per haemodialysis session, under rates effective from 13 October 2025.

Why is SLED dialysis so expensive?

SLED is slow dialysis for very sick patients in the ICU. Yashoda Hospitals lists it at Rs 8,000 to Rs 25,000 per session, compared with Rs 1,500 to Rs 4,000 for routine haemodialysis.
