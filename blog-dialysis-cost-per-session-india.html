Dialysis Cost per Session in India (2026): Private and Government

Most people on dialysis need 2 or 3 sessions a week, so the cost adds up fast. Here is what one session costs, what schemes pay, and what a month costs.

PBy Pulse Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 4 min read
Share

One haemodialysis session in India usually costs about Rs 1,000 to Rs 5,000 at private hospitals and dialysis centres. With 2 or 3 sessions a week, that is about Rs 24,000 to Rs 60,000 a month at the higher end. Dialysis is free for BPL patients at district hospitals under the Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme.

Dialysis Cost per Session at Private Centres

Published price ranges from Indian hospital groups and dialysis chains, checked in November 2026:

SourcePrice per haemodialysis session
NephroPlus (dialysis chain), October 2025About Rs 1,000 – 4,000
Medicover HospitalsRs 2,000 – 5,000
Yashoda Hospitals, conventional haemodialysisRs 1,500 – 4,000

Sources: NephroPlus; Medicover Hospitals; Yashoda Hospitals. NephroPlus puts government-aided centres at about Rs 500 to 1,500 and premium hospitals at up to Rs 4,000.

Cost by Type of Dialysis

Yashoda Hospitals lists these ranges per session for different kinds of haemodialysis (Yashoda Hospitals):

TypePrice per session
Conventional haemodialysisRs 1,500 – 4,000
High-flux haemodialysisRs 3,000 – 7,000
Haemodiafiltration (HDF)Rs 5,000 – 12,000
SLED (slow dialysis in the ICU)Rs 8,000 – 25,000

SLED is slow dialysis for very sick patients in the ICU. That is why it costs much more.

Monthly Cost of Dialysis

Most patients need dialysis 2 or 3 times a week (Medicover). At 3 sessions a week, Medicover puts the cost at Rs 24,000 to Rs 60,000 a month. Home haemodialysis costs Rs 30,000 to Rs 80,000 a month. Our dialysis sessions per week guide explains why the number of sessions matters.

On top of the session fee, budget for:

  • Medicines and injections
  • Regular blood tests
  • Access: an AV fistula or a catheter (see AV fistula vs catheter)
  • Travel to the centre, two or three times a week

One study at a big public hospital in India found patients still paid about Rs 2,838 per session themselves. Most of it (64%) went on medicines and supplies (Clinical Kidney Journal, 2018).

Free Dialysis: Government Programmes

The Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme (PMNDP) started on 7 April 2016. It gives free dialysis to the poor (PMNDP). All BPL patients get haemodialysis free at district hospitals. Peritoneal dialysis was added from 2020 (Rajya Sabha answer, February 2025). Dialysis drugs are also free there (Lok Sabha answer).

  • Andhra Pradesh pays Rs 999 per session under PMNDP. Private hospitals charge Rs 3,000 to Rs 4,000 (ThePrint / PTI).
  • In Madhya Pradesh, BPL patients get dialysis free. Others pay Rs 200 per session (Free Press Journal, September 2026).

To find a centre, ask at your district hospital or check the state-wise list on the PMNDP portal.

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

PM-JAY covers haemodialysis at empanelled hospitals. It is cashless, within the Rs 5 lakh family cover a year (HexaHealth). In April 2026 the rules changed. One approval now covers up to six sessions. Each session needs a fingerprint check at entry and exit, and can last up to four hours (Nagaland IPR, April 2026).

What CGHS Pays per Session

CGHS changed its rates on 13 October 2025. Here are the rates for a Tier II city (CGHS rate list 2025, Y cities):

ProcedureNon-NABHNABH
Haemodialysis, sero-negativeRs 1,913Rs 2,250
Haemodialysis, sero-positiveRs 2,295Rs 2,700
Peritoneal dialysisRs 2,754Rs 3,240
SLEDRs 7,038Rs 8,280

"Sero-positive" means the patient has hepatitis B, hepatitis C or HIV. Tier II rates are 10% below Tier I. Tier III rates are 20% below. Check the list for your city.

How to Lower the Cost

  • Check whether you qualify for free dialysis under PMNDP or a state scheme.
  • Check your PM-JAY, CGHS, ESI or private insurance cover before you start.
  • Compare dialysis centres near you.
  • We suggest asking each centre what the fee includes: dialyser, lines, injections and tests.

Dialysis Cost in India: Key Facts

  • One haemodialysis session costs about Rs 1,000 to Rs 5,000 at private centres in India.
  • With 3 sessions a week, the monthly cost is about Rs 24,000 to Rs 60,000 (Medicover).
  • Dialysis is free for BPL patients at district hospitals under PMNDP, started in April 2016.
  • CGHS pays Rs 1,913 to Rs 2,250 per haemodialysis session in Tier II cities (rates from 13 October 2025).
  • From April 2026, PM-JAY allows up to six dialysis cycles per pre-authorisation, each up to four hours.

Dialysis Cost in India: FAQs

What is the cost of one dialysis session in India?

About Rs 1,000 to Rs 5,000 per haemodialysis session at private hospitals and dialysis centres. NephroPlus puts it at about Rs 1,000 to Rs 4,000, and Medicover at Rs 2,000 to Rs 5,000.

How much does dialysis cost per month in India?

With 3 sessions a week, about Rs 24,000 to Rs 60,000 a month at private centres, according to Medicover. Medicines, tests and travel cost extra.

Is dialysis free in government hospitals?

Yes for BPL patients. Under the Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme, haemodialysis is free for all Below Poverty Line patients at district hospitals, and dialysis drugs are free too. Some states also offer low fees to other patients.

Does Ayushman Bharat cover dialysis?

Yes. Haemodialysis is covered cashless at PM-JAY empanelled hospitals within the Rs 5 lakh family cover. From April 2026, each pre-authorisation covers up to six sessions, with a biometric check at entry and exit.

What does CGHS pay for dialysis?

For a Tier II city, CGHS pays Rs 1,913 (non-NABH) or Rs 2,250 (NABH) per haemodialysis session, under rates effective from 13 October 2025.

Why is SLED dialysis so expensive?

SLED is a slow dialysis given to very sick patients in the ICU, often over many hours. Yashoda Hospitals lists it at Rs 8,000 to Rs 25,000 per session, compared with Rs 1,500 to Rs 4,000 for routine haemodialysis.
