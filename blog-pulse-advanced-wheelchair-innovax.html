Pulse High-Capacity Advanced Wheelchair: Innovax

Most power wheelchairs top out around 100 kg load capacity. For heavier users, or anyone who wants extra safety margin built in, that's a real limitation — not just a number on a spec sheet. Innovax is built specifically to go beyond it.

What is the Pulse Innovax wheelchair? The Pulse Innovax (model WT-100W6016A) is an advanced power wheelchair with a 150 kg load capacity, shock-absorbing springs, turn signals, high-torque dual motors, a 24V lithium battery, and anti-tip wheels — higher capacity and more safety features than Pulse's standard models.

Who the Innovax Is Built For

Innovax suits users who:

  • Need a load capacity above the 100 kg ceiling common on standard power wheelchairs
  • Use the chair regularly on roads or shared paths where turn signals add real safety value
  • Want shock-absorbing suspension for a smoother ride over rougher surfaces
  • Prioritise built-in stability features like anti-tip wheels for extra confidence outdoors

How the Innovax Works in Practice

Innovax's high-torque dual motors and 24V lithium battery deliver a maximum speed of 6 km/h and a range of at least 20 km per charge. The turn signals operate like a standard mobility-vehicle indicator for road and shared-path use, while the anti-tip wheels and shock-absorbing springs work automatically in the background to add stability without requiring any extra input from the user. Armrests are adjustable to fit different users and seating needs.

Why Choose Innovax Over a Standard Model

If load capacity, road safety features, or ride comfort over rough surfaces are priorities, Innovax's spec sheet directly addresses each of them in a way Pulse's lighter or more compact models don't. It's a heavier, more feature-dense chair by design — the trade-off is worthwhile specifically for users who need what those extra features provide.
