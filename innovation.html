Our approach

How we build a better device

We do not start from scratch. We take what already works and make it fit Indian hospitals, budgets and service needs.

  1. Start from proven designs

    We begin with devices that already work well in hospitals.

  2. Make them fit India

    Indian parts where we can, lower cost and easier service.

  3. Add smart features

    Better software, and AI on some devices.

  4. Check, then scale

    Clinical and legal checks before large-scale production.

Research & Development

Designing the next tools for health care.

At our R&D hub in AMTZ, Visakhapatnam, we take proven medical devices from partners and make them better. We switch parts to Indian ones, cut costs, and add better software and AI on some devices. Each device is checked against clinical and legal standards before we make it in large numbers.
