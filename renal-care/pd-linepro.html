How a Blood Tubing Set Is Used in Dialysis

The blood tubing set is the path the blood travels outside the body. The arterial line takes blood from the patient to the blood pump and on to the dialyser. The venous line brings the cleaned blood back to the patient.

Along the way, drip chambers let air bubbles rise out of the blood, and pressure lines connect to the machine so it can watch pressures and stop the pump if something is wrong. Before each session, staff prime the set with saline to push out all the air. Read how a dialysis machine works for the full picture.

What to Check Before Ordering Blood Tubing Sets

  • Machine fit. The pump segment and line layout must suit your dialysis machine. Share your machine model before ordering.
  • Dialyser connection. Check that the line ends fit your dialysers securely.
  • Air removal. Look at the drip chamber design, since trapping air is a key safety job of the set.
  • Priming. Ask how easy the set is to prime, as this affects setup time on busy shifts.
  • Sterilisation and packing. PD-Linepro is sterilised by gamma irradiation or ETO and packed in individual pouches, 25 to an outer pack.
  • Single use. Use a new set for every session.
