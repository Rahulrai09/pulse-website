ADIP Scheme: How to Get a Free or Subsidised Wheelchair

The government ADIP scheme gives wheelchairs, tricycles, hearing aids and other aids to people with disabilities, free or at half cost. This guide explains who qualifies, how much it covers and how to apply.

What Is the ADIP Scheme?

ADIP stands for Assistance to Persons with Disabilities for Purchase or Fitting of Aids and Appliances. It is run by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD). It gives free or low-cost aids such as wheelchairs, tricycles, crutches, hearing aids and artificial limbs.

The scheme was last revised from 26 September 2024. For 2026-27, the government has set aside Rs 375 crore for it and aims to give aids to about 2.43 lakh people this year.

Who Qualifies?

  • You must be an Indian citizen. There is no age limit for most aids.
  • You need a disability certificate of at least 40%, and a UDID card or UDID enrolment number.
  • Family income from all sources must be Rs 30,000 a month or less. For children, the parents' income counts.
  • You must not have received an aid for the same need in the last 3 years. For children under 12, the gap is 1 year.

How Much ADIP Pays

Motorised Wheelchair Under ADIP

To get a motorised wheelchair or motorised tricycle, you need:

  • At least 80% locomotor disability (difficulty moving the body or limbs).
  • Age 16 or over.
  • No motorised wheelchair or tricycle from the scheme in the last 5 years.

People with mental impairment are not eligible for motorised aids. ALIMCO also lists a joystick-controlled motorised wheelchair among the aids that need 80% disability.

How to Apply

  1. Get your UDID card. Apply on the UDID portal if you do not have one. ADIP needs it.
  2. Register on the ARJUN portal (adip.depwd.gov.in). Click Beneficiary Registration, fill in the form, upload your papers as PDF files and submit with the OTP sent to your phone.
  3. Or walk in to an ALIMCO centre, a PM Divyashakti Kendra (PMDK) or an AASRA centre, or ask your district Social Welfare Officer about the next camp.
  4. Attend the camp. Aids are given at district camps, or at the centres of ALIMCO, National Institutes and other agencies.

Papers you need: UDID card or enrolment proof, disability certificate, income certificate (from the Tehsildar, MP, MLA or Gram Pradhan) and Aadhaar card. Add an audiogram for hearing aids.

ALIMCO's toll-free helpline is 1800 180 5129.

Seniors Without a Disability Certificate

A separate scheme, Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY), gives aids to senior citizens. ALIMCO says seniors with monthly income below Rs 15,000 can apply with an Aadhaar card and an income certificate. Call the ALIMCO helpline to ask which aids are available near you.

If You Are Not Eligible or Need More

ADIP gives the aid through a camp or an agency. It is not cash back for a wheelchair you buy in a shop. If your income is above the limit, you need a lighter or more advanced chair, or you cannot wait for a camp, you will buy one yourself. ADIP Scheme Wheelchair: Key Facts

  • ADIP gives free aids to people with 40%+ disability and family income up to Rs 22,500 a month; Rs 22,501 to Rs 30,000 gets 50%.
  • Aids up to Rs 15,000, such as a manual wheelchair, are covered in full for the lower income group.
  • A motorised wheelchair or tricycle gets up to Rs 50,000 for people with 80%+ disability, aged 16 or over, once in 5 years.
  • A UDID card or enrolment number is needed, and you apply on the ARJUN portal or at an ALIMCO centre or camp.
  • For 2026-27, ADIP has Rs 375 crore and aims to reach about 2.43 lakh people.

ADIP Scheme Wheelchair: FAQs

How can I get a free wheelchair from the government?

Under the ADIP scheme, people with at least 40% disability and family income up to Rs 22,500 a month get aids such as a wheelchair free, up to Rs 15,000. Register on the ARJUN portal or visit an ALIMCO centre or camp.

Who is eligible for a motorised wheelchair under ADIP?

People with at least 80% locomotor disability, aged 16 or over, can get up to Rs 50,000 for a motorised wheelchair or tricycle, once in 5 years.

What is the income limit for the ADIP scheme?

Family income must be Rs 30,000 a month or less. Up to Rs 22,500 gets the full cost; Rs 22,501 to Rs 30,000 gets 50%.

Is a UDID card needed for ADIP?

Yes. You need a UDID card or UDID enrolment number along with a disability certificate of at least 40%.

How often can I get a wheelchair under ADIP?

Not for the same need within 3 years, or 1 year for children under 12. A motorised wheelchair or tricycle can be given once in 5 years.
