How Many Labour Tables Do You Need?

Start with your delivery load. The Government of India's labour room guidelines link the number of tables to deliveries per month.

Deliveries per monthLabour tables
Fewer than 201
20 to 992
100 to 1994 (conventional labour room)
200 to 4996 (conventional labour room)
500 or moreWorked out by formula, for LDR or conventional rooms

Most small nursing homes need one or two tables. A busy maternity hospital or district hospital needs four to six, or a set of LDR rooms. In an LDR (labour, delivery and recovery) room, the mother stays in one room from active labour until about 4 hours after birth.

Add a wall clock with a seconds hand in each labour room and newborn corner, a fridge for oxytocin and other drugs, and instrument trays for normal delivery.

Focus lights are extra. Mobile examination lights start at about Rs 11,000 each, so four add at least Rs 44,000. The total also leaves out small items such as the BP monitor, scales and delivery instrument sets.

For a small room with 2 tables, 1 CTG monitor, 2 Dopplers, 1 warmer, 1 suction unit and 1 autoclave, the same prices give about Rs 1.6 lakh to Rs 17.5 lakh.

Two choices move the total the most. A hydraulic delivery table costs a fraction of an electric one. A hospital with its own CSSD may not need a separate labour room autoclave at all.

For a government reference, Tamil Nadu Medical Services Corporation opened bids for CTG monitors in December 2024. Wired models were quoted at Rs 69,400 to Rs 1.23 lakh, and the two lowest wireless models at Rs 1.35 lakh and Rs 1.68 lakh, before GST.

Space and Layout Rules

  • Table spacing: keep each table at least 3 ft from the side wall and 2 ft from the head wall, with 6 ft between tables.
  • LDR rooms: each labour area is about 10 ft by 10 ft, and one LDR unit has 4 beds.
  • Newborn care area: one for every 4 labour tables, with a radiant warmer, resuscitation kit and oxygen.
  • Toilet: an attached toilet of about 6 ft by 8 ft for the labour room.
  • Support rooms: a staff changing room of about 15 ft by 10 ft, a nursing station, a triage area and a waiting area for families.

Plan oxygen and suction points, power backup and air conditioning before the civil work starts. Moving them later costs far more.

Staff for a Labour Room

Staff nurses run most labour rooms. The guidelines ask for 4 staff nurses for each LDR unit: one for each shift and one as backup. In a district hospital, staff nurses conduct the normal deliveries, with an obstetrician, a trained medical officer and an anaesthetist on call for problems.

Private hospitals should also plan for a paediatrician on call at every birth that may need help, and a trained nurse for newborn care.

LaQshya and Quality Rules

LaQshya is the Ministry of Health's labour room quality programme, launched on 11 December 2017. It covers labour rooms and maternity operation theatres in government hospitals. It asks hospitals to lay out their labour rooms as per the standard guidelines and to follow safe birth checklists.

LaQshya is for government hospitals, but its layout and equipment norms are a good benchmark for any private labour room.

How to Plan and Save

  • Size the room for the deliveries you expect in three years, not today.
  • Choose hydraulic tables for normal case loads; buy electric tables where staff move patients often.
  • Share one CTG monitor between two tables at first, and add more as the load grows.
  • Use the hospital CSSD for sterilising if you have one.
  • Plan the medical gas pipeline with the building, not after it.
  • Ask for a service contract and spare parts for every table, warmer and monitor.

Labour Room Setup Cost in India: FAQs

How much does it cost to set up a labour room in India?

A labour room with 4 delivery tables needs about Rs 2.7 lakh to Rs 24 lakh of core equipment, by our estimate from 2026 prices, plus focus lights. A 2-table room needs about Rs 1.6 lakh to Rs 17.5 lakh. Building work, air conditioning and the gas pipeline are extra.

How many labour tables does a hospital need?

Government labour room guidelines suggest 1 table for under 20 deliveries a month, 2 tables for 20 to 99, 4 for 100 to 199 and 6 for 200 to 499. Larger units work out the number of LDR or labour beds by formula.

What equipment is needed in a labour room?

The core list is a delivery table and focus light for each table, a radiant warmer and resuscitation kit for every 4 tables, fetal Dopplers, a CTG monitor, suction, an autoclave, oxygen, a BP monitor, a baby weighing scale, wheelchairs and stretchers.

What is an LDR room?

LDR stands for labour, delivery and recovery. The mother stays in one room from active labour until about 4 hours after birth. Indian guidelines group 4 LDR beds into one unit, each labour area about 10 ft by 10 ft.

What is LaQshya?

LaQshya is the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare labour room quality programme, launched on 11 December 2017. It aims to improve care in labour rooms and maternity operation theatres in government hospitals.
