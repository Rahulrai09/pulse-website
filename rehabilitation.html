Choosing the Right Pulse Wheelchair

Pulse makes 11 electric wheelchair models in five categories. Each one is built for a different need: rough terrain, daily portability, heavy loads, extra rest support, or travel-friendly folding. Here is a quick guide to each range:

  • Motion Pro-1 & 2 — heavy-duty steel-frame wheelchairs that carry users up to 140 kg; the Motion Pro 2 can also be pushed by hand
  • Aerodrive-1 & 2 — folding power wheelchairs for everyday travel; the Aerodrive 1 reclines to 160°
  • Joylite-1 & 2 — lightweight folding wheelchairs at just 19 kg without the battery
  • Innovax — everyday folding power wheelchair that folds with one click
  • Cruza — reclining power wheelchair for extended rest support
  • Xtrion — compact X-fold wheelchair with puncture-free tyres
  • Smartride-1 & 2 — aluminium-frame folding wheelchairs for travel: one-click fold (Smartride 1) or remote autofold (Smartride 2)

For a full breakdown of what to consider — battery range, load capacity, frame weight, and use case — see our Rehabilitation Equipment Manufacturer guide.
