How Much Does Angioplasty Cost in India?

Angioplasty opens a blocked heart artery with a small balloon, and usually leaves a stent inside to keep it open. The price depends on the hospital, the city, the number of stents and how long you stay.

What Makes Up the Angioplasty Bill?

An angioplasty bill has five main parts:

  • Angiogram: a dye test that shows where the artery is blocked. It is often done just before the angioplasty.
  • Procedure and cath lab charges: the special X-ray room, the team and the doctor's fee.
  • Stent: the tiny mesh tube left in the artery. Its price is capped by law (see below).
  • Other single-use items: a sheath to enter the artery, a guiding catheter, a guidewire, one or more balloons, an inflation device and sometimes a closure device to seal the entry point.
  • Stay and medicines: ICU or ward days, tests and blood thinners.

The Stent Price Cap

The government body NPPA sets the highest price for a heart stent. From 1 April 2026, a drug-eluting stent can cost at most Rs 39,186.03 and a bare metal stent Rs 10,762.15, plus GST. The cap is on the stent only, not on the rest of the bill. Your bill should show the stent's brand, batch number and price on a separate line. Read more in our stent price guide.

One Stent vs Two Stents

Each extra stent adds its own price, often one more balloon, and more time in the cath lab. Under PM-JAY, the hospital is paid Rs 34,128 for each drug-eluting stent used. In a private hospital, a second drug-eluting stent can add up to Rs 39,186 plus GST for the stent alone. Ask your doctor before the procedure how many stents may be needed, and ask the hospital to price both cases.

What Government Schemes Pay

PM-JAY (Ayushman Bharat): eligible families get free hospital care of up to Rs 5 lakh a year. Since 29 October 2024, every person aged 70 or over can also join through the Ayushman Vay Vandana Card, whatever their income. Under the 2022 package list, the hospital is paid Rs 40,600 as a base rate for angioplasty with the angiogram, Rs 50,800 to Rs 60,900 by city, plus the stent.

CGHS: for central government staff and pensioners, rates from 13 October 2025 pay a metro NABH hospital Rs 97,000 for angioplasty with a closure device and Rs 83,725 without one. Smaller cities are paid less, and non-NABH hospitals 15% less. The stent is paid on top. CGHS counts guidewires and catheters as part of the package and plans up to 3 days in hospital for a planned angioplasty.

Private insurance: most health policies cover angioplasty, but some have a waiting period for heart problems. Check your policy and ask the hospital for a cashless estimate before you are admitted.

Questions to Ask the Hospital

  • Is the quote a package? What does it include, and what is extra?
  • Which stent will you use, and what is its price on the bill?
  • What happens to the price if a second stent is needed?
  • Is a closure device included, or charged separately?
  • How many days in the ICU and ward does the quote include?
  • Do you accept PM-JAY, CGHS or my insurance for this?

Angioplasty Cost in India: FAQs

What is the cost of angioplasty in India?

At private hospitals, angioplasty costs about Rs 1.5 lakh to Rs 4.5 lakh, and one health platform puts the average at about Rs 1.63 lakh. The price depends on the city, the hospital, the number of stents and the length of stay.

What is the price of a stent in angioplasty?

From 1 April 2026, the legal maximum price is Rs 39,186.03 for a drug-eluting stent and Rs 10,762.15 for a bare metal stent, plus GST. The cap covers only the stent.

Is angioplasty free under Ayushman Bharat?

Yes, for eligible families at empanelled hospitals, within the Rs 5 lakh yearly cover. People aged 70 and over can join through the Ayushman Vay Vandana Card, whatever their income.

How much does CGHS pay for angioplasty?

From 13 October 2025, CGHS pays a metro NABH hospital Rs 97,000 for angioplasty with a closure device and Rs 83,725 without one. The stent is paid on top.

How much does a second stent add?

A second drug-eluting stent can add up to Rs 39,186 plus GST for the stent alone, plus more balloons and cath lab time. Ask the hospital to price both one-stent and two-stent cases.
