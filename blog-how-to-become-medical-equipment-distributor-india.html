How to Become a Medical Equipment Distributor or Dealer in India

Hospitals, clinics and labs across India buy through local dealers. This guide explains the licence you need, the rules on storage and records, how to pick brands and the steps to start.

What Does a Medical Equipment Distributor Do?

A distributor or dealer buys medical devices from makers or importers and sells them to hospitals, clinics, labs and shops in an area. Good dealers also show products, set them up, train staff, answer service calls and keep stock close to buyers. Some sell only consumables; others sell machines that need service.

The Licence You Need: MD-42

Since 30 September 2022, anyone who sells, stocks or supplies medical devices, including lab devices, needs a sale licence or a certificate in Form MD-42.

Storage and Record Rules

  • Store each device as its label says. Lab reagents often need 2 to 8 °C with a temperature log.
  • Keep sterile items off the floor, away from damp and sun. Lock away implants and costly items.
  • Send out the stock that expires first, and keep expired or recalled stock apart.
  • Keep buying and selling records with batch numbers for at least two years, plus an inspection book (Form MD-43).
  • Buy only from licensed makers, importers or sellers, and keep copies of their licences.

Selling without a licence is a crime under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, with jail and fines. So register before you stock anything.

How to Choose Brands to Distribute

  • Licences: check the maker's or importer's CDSCO licence for each product. See our CDSCO licence guide.
  • Certificates: ISO 13485, and CE where it applies, matter for hospital and government tenders.
  • Service: who sets up, trains and repairs, and how fast spare parts arrive.
  • Range: machines plus repeat consumables give steady income.
  • Terms: get the area, margin, credit days, minimum order and sales help in writing.

Steps to Start a Distributorship

  1. Pick your customers and area: hospitals, clinics, labs or home users, in which cities.
  2. Pick products you can explain and service, such as ICU, dialysis, lab or hospital furniture.
  3. Set up the firm, premises and storage, and register for GST.
  4. Apply for MD-42 in Form MD-41 with a competent person named.
  5. Sign distributor agreements with licensed brands.
  6. Meet hospital buyers and biomedical engineers. Register on GeM if you will sell to government hospitals. See our GeM guide.

Dealer or Direct?

Many hospitals now buy big machines direct from makers and use local dealers for consumables, small devices and fast service. See our medical equipment distributors in India guide for how hospitals buy. A dealer who adds real local service stays valuable either way.

Become a Medical Equipment Distributor: Key Facts

  • Since 30 September 2022, anyone who sells, stocks or distributes medical devices in India needs a sale licence or an MD-42 registration certificate.
  • You apply in Form MD-41 to the state licensing authority; the fee is Rs 3,000 and grant takes about 10 days.
  • MD-42 has no fixed expiry, but a Rs 3,000 retention fee is due every five years.
  • Each stocking premises needs its own registration; a drug sale licence ordinarily covers devices.
  • Purchase and sale records with batch numbers must be kept for at least two years.

Become a Medical Equipment Distributor: FAQs

What licence is needed to sell medical equipment in India?

A registration certificate in Form MD-42 from your state licensing authority, unless you already hold a drug sale or wholesale licence. It has been required since 30 September 2022.

What is the fee for an MD-42 registration?

Rs 3,000. The certificate has no fixed expiry, but a retention fee of Rs 3,000 is due every five years, with a late fee for delays.

How long does MD-42 registration take?

About 10 days after a complete application in Form MD-41. Missing documents lead to queries and delays.

Do chemists need MD-42 to sell medical devices?

Ordinarily not. A drug sale or wholesale licence under the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules generally covers stocking and selling medical devices. Check with your state authority.

What records must a medical device distributor keep?

Purchase and sale registers with device name, batch, quantity, supplier and buyer for at least two years, an inspection book in Form MD-43, and copies of suppliers' licences.
