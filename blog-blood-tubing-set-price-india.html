What Is in a Blood Tubing Set?

  • Arterial line (red): takes blood from the patient's needle or catheter to the blood pump and on to the dialyser.
  • Venous line (blue): brings the cleaned blood back to the patient.
  • Pump segment: a thicker piece of tube that sits in the machine's roller pump.
  • Drip chambers: let air bubbles rise out of the blood before it goes back to the patient.
  • Pressure lines: link to the machine through transducer protectors so it can watch pressures. See our transducer protector price guide.
  • Side lines: for heparin, saline and priming, with clamps and caps.

What to Check Before You Buy

A Gujarat government tender on GeM (June 2026) shows the specs most buyers ask for:

SpecWhat the GeM catalogue asks for
Patient typeAdult
Tubing typePost-pump
PartsArterial and venous lines
Colour codingYes
MaterialMedical-grade PVC
Tube length100 cm or more, within 5%
Priming volume150 ml or less
  • Machine fit: the pump segment and line layout must suit your dialysis machine. Share your machine model with the supplier before ordering.
  • Pre-pump or post-pump: this tells you where the arterial drip chamber sits compared with the blood pump. Buy the type your machine and unit protocol use.
  • Priming volume: this is the blood or saline needed to fill the set. Smaller is better for small or unwell patients. India's dialysis programme lists 40 to 50 ml tubing for children.
  • Sterile and single use: check the sterilisation method (gamma or ETO) and that each set is packed on its own.
  • Air removal and kinks: look at the drip chamber design and ask for kink-resistant tubing.

Can Blood Tubing Sets Be Reused?

No. The 2026 Operational Guidelines for the Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme say blood lines, transducer protectors, IV sets and catheters should not be reused. The same guidelines let a dialyser be reused up to 5 times for the same patient, but never for patients with hepatitis B, hepatitis C or HIV.

So plan your budget for one new set every session, even if your unit reuses dialysers.

Blood Tubing Set Price in India: FAQs

What is the price of a blood tubing set in India?

A haemodialysis blood tubing set costs about Rs 103 to Rs 135 per set when bought in bulk packs, based on Medikabazaar listings in November 2026. Small lots from local dealers cost about Rs 180 to Rs 320 per set.

How many blood tubing sets does a dialysis patient need?

One set is used per session. A patient on dialysis three times a week needs 156 sets a year. A 10-machine unit running 3 shifts for 26 days a month needs about 780 sets a month.

Can blood tubing sets be reused?

No. The 2026 Operational Guidelines for the Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme say blood lines, transducer protectors, IV sets and catheters should not be reused. Use a new set for every session.

What is the priming volume of a blood tubing set?

Adult sets bought on GeM are asked to have a priming volume of 150 ml or less. India's national dialysis programme lists 40 to 50 ml tubing for children.

What is the difference between pre-pump and post-pump blood lines?

The name tells you where the arterial drip chamber sits compared with the blood pump: before it in a pre-pump set and after it in a post-pump set. Buy the type your machine and unit protocol use.
