Single Dome or Double Dome?

A single dome is enough for minor surgery and smaller OTs. A double dome (main and satellite) lights the field from two angles, which reduces shadows when surgeons lean over the wound, and gives a back-up if one dome fails. Most major OTs choose double dome.

Specs That Matter

1. Light intensity (lux)

Surgical lights commonly deliver well over 100,000 lux at the centre of the field. More is not always better: adjustable intensity matters for comfort during long surgeries.

2. Field size and focus

Adjustable focus lets the team widen the light for large incisions or narrow it for deep cavities.

3. Shadow control and colour

Multiple LEDs and reflectors reduce shadows. Good colour rendering helps surgeons tell tissues apart.

4. LED life and heat

LED lights run cooler than halogen and last tens of thousands of hours, which lowers running costs.

5. Mounting, camera and battery

Ceiling mounting needs structural planning. Integrated HD cameras and battery backup add cost but help teaching and power cuts.

OT Light Price in India: FAQs

How much does an OT light cost in India?

Online listings in October 2026 show LED single dome ceiling OT lights from about Rs 35,000, double dome lights at about Rs 60,000 to Rs 1.65 lakh, and mobile OT lights from about Rs 11,000. Premium lights with HD cameras cost more.

What is the difference between a single dome and double dome OT light?

A single dome is one light head. A double dome adds a second (satellite) head, so the surgical field is lit from two angles with fewer shadows, and one head can keep working if the other fails.

How many lux does an OT light need?

Surgical lights commonly deliver well over 100,000 lux at the centre of the field, with adjustable intensity. The Pulse Four Reflector and Globus Dome lights are rated at 180,000 lux.

Is LED better than halogen for OT lights?

Usually yes. LED lights run cooler, use less power and last tens of thousands of hours, so they cost less to run than halogen lights.

What should I check before buying an OT light?

Check intensity and adjustability, field size and focus, shadow control, colour rendering, LED life, single or double dome, ceiling structure for mounting, battery backup, camera option and local service.
