Light Frames vs Heavy-Duty Frames

The Joylite, Smartride, Aerodrive and Innovax models use aluminium frames and 10 or 12Ah batteries. They are easier to lift and store, and suit users up to 120 kg. The Xtrion has a steel frame in a compact X-fold. The Cruza and Motion Pro models also use steel frames, with larger 20Ah batteries. They weigh more but carry users up to 140 kg. We suggest picking the lightest model that safely carries the user, because someone will have to lift it.

Pulse Electric Wheelchair Models: FAQs

How many electric wheelchair models does Pulse make?

Pulse makes 11 models: Joylite 1 and 2, Smartride 1 and 2, Aerodrive 1 and 2, Xtrion, Innovax, Motion Pro 1 and 2, and Cruza.

Which is the lightest Pulse electric wheelchair?

The Joylite 1 and Joylite 2 weigh 19 kg without the battery. The Joylite 1 also has a removable battery and puncture-free tyres.

Which Pulse wheelchair is best for a heavy user?

The Cruza, Motion Pro 1 and Motion Pro 2 carry users up to 140 kg and have steel frames and 24V 20Ah batteries. Other models carry up to 120 kg.

Which Pulse electric wheelchairs recline?

The Aerodrive 1 and the Cruza recline to 160 degrees and have a headrest. The Cruza reclines by remote.

What is the range and speed of Pulse electric wheelchairs?

All models have a top speed of 6 km/h and a range of about 15 km per charge. Range depends on the user's weight and the ground.
