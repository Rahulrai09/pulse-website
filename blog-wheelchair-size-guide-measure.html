Wheelchair Size Guide: How to Measure for the Right Fit

A wheelchair that is too wide, too narrow or too deep causes discomfort, poor posture and pressure sores. This guide shows the measurements to take, how they turn into a seat size, and the seat sizes of Pulse wheelchairs.

PBy Pulse Team · Updated November 25, 2026 · 4 min read
Share

Measure the user sitting upright. Seat width = hip width at the widest point of the hips or thighs. Seat depth = back of the pelvis to the back of the knee, minus 30 to 60 mm, using the shorter leg. Calf length (back of the knee to the heel, in usual shoes) sets the footrest height.

Why Size Matters

A seat that is too wide makes the user lean to one side and makes the wheels harder to reach. One that is too narrow presses on the hips. A seat that is too deep presses behind the knees, and one that is too short puts more weight on the buttocks, which raises the risk of pressure sores. See our bedsore prevention guide.

How to Measure

Sit the user upright on a firm chair, with the pelvis level and pockets empty. Use a metal tape measure.

Tips Before You Buy

  • Measure in the clothes the user wears most days.
  • If one leg is shorter, size the seat depth to the shorter leg.
  • Check the user's weight against the chair's weight limit.
  • We suggest measuring your narrowest door, lift and car boot too, so the chair fits your home and travel.
  • Add a pressure-relief cushion for anyone who sits for long hours.

Wheelchair Size Guide: Key Facts

  • Seat width should match the user's hip width at the widest point.
  • Seat depth is back of pelvis to back of knee, minus 30 to 60 mm.
  • Calf length, measured in usual shoes, sets the footrest height.
  • Backrest height depends on how much back support the user needs.
  • Pulse wheelchair seats range from 42 x 40 cm to 50 x 45 cm.

Wheelchair Size Guide: FAQs

How do I measure wheelchair seat width?

Measure across the widest part of the hips or thighs while seated. The seat width should equal this hip width.

How do I measure wheelchair seat depth?

Measure from the back of the pelvis to the back of the knee, then take away 30 to 60 mm. Use the shorter leg.

What happens if a wheelchair is too wide?

The user tends to lean to one side and has to reach further for the wheels, which is tiring and harms posture.

How is footrest height set?

Measure from the back of the knee to the heel in usual shoes. This sets the distance from the top of the cushion to the footrest.

What seat sizes do Pulse wheelchairs have?

From 42 x 40 cm (Joylite 2) to 50 x 45 cm (Aerodrive), with weight limits of 120 or 140 kg.
