Pulse Premium Comfort & TravelMate Lightweight Power Wheelchairs

Between a heavy-duty all-terrain chair and an ultra-light travel model sits a genuinely useful middle ground: a wheelchair light enough to handle daily without a struggle, but capable enough for real outdoor use. That's the Pulse Premium Comfort and TravelMate.

PBy Pulse Team · Updated October 15, 2026 · 5 min read
Share

What are the Pulse Premium Comfort and TravelMate? The Pulse Premium Comfort and Pulse TravelMate are folding electric wheelchairs with aluminium frames and a wide 50 cm seat. Pulse Premium Comfort (27 kg without battery) reclines up to 160° and has a headrest and suspension. Pulse TravelMate (23 kg) folds in seconds and rolls like a trolley. Both carry up to 120 kg and run about 15 km per charge.

Ideal For: Premium Comfort and TravelMate Users

The Pulse Premium Comfort and TravelMate suit users who:

  • Need a chair that is easier to lift and load than a heavy-duty model
  • Have a family member or carer who often moves the chair
  • Want a wide, comfortable 50 cm seat
  • Weigh up to 120 kg

Pulse Premium Comfort or Pulse TravelMate?

Pulse Premium Comfort (27 kg without battery) is the comfort model. Its backrest reclines up to 160°, nearly flat, for rest or care. It also has an adjustable headrest, flip-up armrests, suspension, amber indicator lights and anti-tip support, and runs on a 24V 12Ah battery.

Pulse TravelMate (23 kg without battery) is the travel model. It folds in seconds and rolls along like a trolley, with a breathable mesh seat and a 24V 10Ah battery.

Both have aluminium frames, two 250 W motors, a top speed of 6 km/h, about 15 km of range per charge and electromagnetic brakes.

Pulse Lightweight Power Wheelchair: Key Facts

  • Pulse Premium Comfort uses a 24V 12Ah battery (about 288 Wh); Pulse TravelMate uses a 24V 10Ah battery (about 240 Wh).
  • Both batteries are under the 300 Wh airline limit for a removed wheelchair battery.
  • Pulse TravelMate has a one-hand 360 degree joystick.
  • WHO's 2023 Wheelchair Provision Guidelines recommend a personal assessment, proper fitting, user training and follow-up for every wheelchair user.

Flying with the Pulse Premium Comfort and TravelMate

Airlines care about how much energy a wheelchair battery stores, measured in watt-hours (Wh). You work it out as volts × amp-hours. The Pulse Premium Comfort and TravelMate use a 24V 12Ah battery (Pulse Premium Comfort) or a 24V 10Ah battery (Pulse TravelMate), which is about 288 (Pulse Premium Comfort) and 240 (Pulse TravelMate) Wh. That is under the 300 Wh limit that airline safety rules set for a battery removed from a wheelchair, which makes air travel simpler.

Always tell your airline before you fly. Removed and spare batteries must go in the cabin with their terminals covered. You may carry one spare battery up to 300 Wh, or two spares up to 160 Wh each.

Pulse Lightweight Power Wheelchair: FAQs

Who should choose the Pulse Premium Comfort and TravelMate?

Users who want a wide-seat electric wheelchair that is easier to lift and load than a heavy-duty chair, up to 120 kg, including families and carers who move the chair often.

How much do the Pulse Premium Comfort and TravelMate weigh?

Without the battery, Pulse Premium Comfort weighs 27 kg and Pulse TravelMate weighs 23 kg. Both have aluminium frames.

What's the difference between Pulse Premium Comfort and Pulse TravelMate?

Pulse Premium Comfort reclines up to 160 degrees and adds a headrest, flip-up armrests, suspension and indicator lights. Pulse TravelMate is lighter and folds in seconds to roll like a trolley. Both carry 120 kg and run about 15 km per charge.
