How much does a stretcher trolley cost in India?

Price depends mostly on the type. A plain trolley with a fixed height is cheap. A trolley that lifts, tilts and carries an oxygen cylinder costs four to five times more.

What should an emergency trolley spec include?

A GeM listing for a hydraulic emergency recovery trolley shows what a full spec looks like. Use it as a checklist when you compare quotes:

  • Safe load: 250 kg.
  • Height: 650 to 950 mm, raised by a hydraulic pump.
  • Tilt: Trendelenburg (head down) and reverse Trendelenburg (head up), up to 25 degrees.
  • Back rest: lifts up to 75 degrees.
  • Castors: four 150 mm wheels, two with locks.
  • Side rails: stainless steel, fold down for transfers.
  • Add-ons: IV pole, oxygen cylinder holder, X-ray see-through top with a cassette holder.
  • Safety: a fail-safe that stops the top from dropping if a gas spring fails.

You may not need every item. But ask each supplier to fill in the same list, so you compare like with like.

Mild steel or stainless steel?

Most trolleys come in two frame types.

  • MS (mild steel), powder coated: costs less and is strong. The coating can chip, and rust can start where it does.
  • SS (stainless steel): costs more but stands up better to daily cleaning with disinfectants. It is a good choice for emergency rooms, OTs and wet areas.

Many buyers choose a mild steel frame with a stainless steel top. It saves money where it matters least.

What drives the price?

  • Height system: fixed, manual crank or hydraulic. Hydraulic adds the most cost.
  • Material: stainless steel costs more than mild steel.
  • Castors: bigger wheels with good brakes roll better and cost more.
  • Mattress and rails: a thick, washable mattress and folding rails add cost.
  • Add-ons: IV pole, oxygen holder, X-ray top and tilt.
  • Quantity and service: bulk orders cost less per unit. A warranty and spare parts matter more than a small discount.

Stretcher Trolley Price in India: FAQs

How much does a stretcher trolley cost in India?

A basic stretcher on trolley costs about Rs 8,500 to Rs 12,000, and a deluxe trolley with mattress, rails and IV pole costs about Rs 20,000 to Rs 35,000 (Medikabazaar, November 2026). Hydraulic emergency trolleys cost more.

What is the price of a hydraulic emergency trolley in India?

Hydraulic emergency and recovery trolleys cost about Rs 31,500 to Rs 56,000 in online listings (Medikabazaar, TradeIndia and GeM, November 2026). Premium models can cost up to about Rs 1.9 lakh.

What is the difference between a stretcher and a stretcher trolley?

A stretcher is carried by hand and has no wheels, like a folding or scoop stretcher. A stretcher trolley has a frame on castors, so one or two staff can wheel the patient between wards, the OT and X-ray.

How much weight should a hospital stretcher trolley carry?

Check the rated load on the maker's spec sheet. A full-spec hydraulic emergency trolley on GeM is rated for 250 kg. For heavier patients, ask for a bariatric model.

Should I buy a mild steel or stainless steel stretcher trolley?

Powder-coated mild steel costs less and suits light use. Stainless steel costs more but resists rust from daily cleaning, so it suits emergency rooms, OTs and wet areas. A mild steel frame with a steel top is a common middle path.
