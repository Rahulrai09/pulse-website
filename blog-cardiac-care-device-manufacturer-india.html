What Counts as Cardiac Care Equipment

This group covers the devices used in the cath lab to open and treat heart arteries. It includes:

  • Drug-eluting coronary stents (sirolimus, everolimus)
  • Inflation device kits and manifolds
  • Guidewires
  • Vascular access needles
  • Sheath kits that give safe access to a vein or artery

Stents and other devices that stay in the body are usually in CDSCO's top risk group, Class D. So proof from trials and proof of good factory quality matter more here than for almost any other kind of equipment.

Why This Category Demands Extra Scrutiny

A stent stays in the patient for life. If it fails, the harm is direct and lasting. Unlike a monitor or a ventilator, you cannot swap a stent for another brand halfway through a case. So trial data, strut design and how firmly the stent holds on the balloon really matter. These are not sales points. Your heart doctors should read them before a stent is added to your cath lab list.

What to Verify Before Buying

  • CDSCO licence and risk class (most cath lab devices are Class C or D)
  • Trial or test data, above all for stents and other devices left in the body
  • Proof that the device is sterile and safe for body contact
  • ISO 13485 certificate, and which plant and products it covers
  • Who will support your team in the cath lab during cases
  • How each batch is tracked, and how a recall would work
For implantable cardiac devices, ask for the clinical evidence before you ask for the price. Here, the cheaper choice is not always the safe one.

Cardiac Care Device Manufacturer in India: Key Facts

  • Cardiac care devices include coronary stents, guidewires, inflation kits, manifolds, access needles and introducer kits.
  • Coronary stents and other implantable devices usually fall in CDSCO's highest risk tier, Class D.
  • From 1 April 2026, the NPPA ceiling price for a drug-eluting stent is Rs 39,186.03 plus GST.
  • State Licensing Authorities license Class A and B manufacturing; the Central Licensing Authority regulates Class C and D.
  • Pulse makes two drug-eluting stents: Evera (everolimus) and Sirova (sirolimus).

Cardiac Care Device Manufacturer in India: FAQs

Who manufactures cardiac care devices in India?

India's cardiac care device manufacturers include Meril Life Sciences and horizontal OEMs such as Pulse. Between them, they produce coronary stents, PTCA balloons, guidewires, sheaths, and cath lab accessories. Each manufacturer should be verified individually against their CDSCO licence and certification before purchase.

What cardiac care devices are manufactured in India?

Indian manufacturers produce drug-eluting coronary stents, inflation device kits, manifolds, guidewires, vascular access needles, and introducer/sheath kits used in interventional cardiology and cath lab procedures.

What should a hospital check before buying cardiac devices from an Indian manufacturer?

Check the device's CDSCO risk classification (most interventional cardiac devices are Class C or D), the manufacturer's current licence, clinical evidence for stents and implantable devices, sterility and biocompatibility documentation, and cath lab technical support availability.
