Cardiac Care Device Manufacturer in India: Stents, Guidewires & Cath Lab Equipment

Interventional cardiology devices carry some of the highest regulatory scrutiny of any medical equipment category — and for good reason. Here's who manufactures cardiac care devices in India, and what to verify before your cath lab commits to a supplier.

Who manufactures cardiac care devices in India? India's cardiac care device manufacturers include Meril Life Sciences and horizontal OEMs such as Pulse, producing coronary stents, PTCA balloons, guidewires, sheaths, and cath lab accessories. Each manufacturer should be verified individually against their CDSCO licence and certification before purchase.

What Counts as Cardiac Care Equipment

This category covers the devices used in interventional cardiology and cath lab procedures, including:

  • Drug-eluting coronary stents (sirolimus, everolimus)
  • Inflation device kits and manifolds
  • Guidewires
  • Vascular access needles
  • Introducer and sheath kits for venous and arterial access

Coronary stents and other implantable devices typically fall into CDSCO's highest risk tier (Class D), which means clinical evidence and manufacturing quality documentation matter more here than in almost any other equipment category.

Why This Category Demands Extra Scrutiny

Implantable devices like drug-eluting stents carry direct, long-term clinical consequences if something goes wrong. Unlike a monitor or ventilator, a stent can't be swapped out for a different brand mid-procedure. This is the category where clinical evidence, strut design data, and dislodgement force specifications genuinely matter — not just as marketing copy, but as information your interventional cardiologists should actually review before a device enters your cath lab formulary.

What to Verify Before Buying

  • CDSCO manufacturing licence and device classification (typically Class C or D for interventional devices)
  • Clinical evaluation or performance evidence for stents and implantable devices specifically
  • Sterility and biocompatibility documentation
  • ISO 13485 certificate and its exact facility/product scope
  • Cath lab technical support availability during procedures
  • Batch traceability and recall process documentation
For implantable cardiac devices, ask for the clinical evidence before you ask for the price. This is not a category where the cheaper option is automatically the safer trade-off.
