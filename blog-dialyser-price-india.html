Dialyser Price in India and Dialysis Consumables Cost per Session

Every haemodialysis session uses a dialyser, blood tubing, needles or a catheter and other single-use items. This guide lists typical prices item by item, the total consumables cost per session, and what dialysis units should check before choosing a supplier.

What a Dialyser Does

The dialyser is the filter at the heart of haemodialysis. Blood flows through thousands of thin hollow fibres while dialysate flows around them. Waste such as urea moves from the blood into the dialysate, and the machine pulls extra water out across the same membrane. Read how a dialysis machine works for the full picture.

Low-Flux or High-Flux?

Low-flux dialysers let smaller molecules pass and cost a little less. High-flux dialysers have larger pores that also clear some larger molecules and allow more water removal. The choice is made by the nephrologist for each patient. Dialysers also come in different membrane surface areas (for example 1.4 or 1.6 square metres); larger areas clear more per session.

What Drives Consumables Cost

1. Single use or reuse

Some units in India reprocess dialysers for the same patient under a written policy. This lowers the dialyser cost per session but adds reprocessing work, testing and records.

2. Access type

Patients with a fistula need a pair of needles each session. Patients without one need a haemodialysis catheter, which costs more but is not replaced every session.

3. Brand and membrane

Membrane material, surface area and flux change the dialyser price.

4. Buying in volume

Units that order monthly in bulk, with a fixed set from one supplier, usually get better prices and fewer stock gaps.

Dialyser Price in India and Dialysis Consumables Cost per Session: FAQs

How much does a dialyser cost in India?

Online listings show single dialysers at about Rs 620 to Rs 1,250 each, depending on brand, membrane, surface area and flux. Bulk orders for a dialysis unit are usually priced separately.

What is the total consumables cost per dialysis session?

SMD Medicare's September 2026 guide puts total consumables at about Rs 1,100 to Rs 1,800 per session, depending on whether dialysers are used once or reprocessed.

What consumables are used in each haemodialysis session?

A dialyser, a blood tubing set, AV fistula needles or a catheter connection, acid and bicarbonate concentrate, heparin, transducer protectors and disinfectants.

What is the difference between low-flux and high-flux dialysers?

High-flux dialysers have larger pores that clear some larger molecules and allow more water removal. Low-flux dialysers cost a little less. The nephrologist chooses for each patient.

Can dialysers be reused?

Some units in India reprocess dialysers for the same patient under a written reuse policy with testing and records. Others use each dialyser once. Follow your unit's policy and the maker's instructions.
