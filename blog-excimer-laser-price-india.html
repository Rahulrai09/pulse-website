What Is an Excimer 308 nm Machine?

An excimer machine gives narrow-band UVB light at 308 nm only on the patch being treated, not on the whole body. It is used for localised vitiligo, psoriasis plaques and patchy hair loss (alopecia areata). Sessions take seconds to a few minutes and are usually given once or twice a week.

Excimer Lamp vs Excimer Laser

Both give 308 nm light. A laser gives a small, very intense spot; a lamp (also called excimer light) gives a larger spot at lower power and costs less. Most machines sold to Indian clinics in this price range are lamps. Ask the seller which one you are buying and check the stated wavelength, as some listings mix up the specs.

What Does the Evidence Say?

A 2016 meta-analysis of 6 randomised trials (235 patients) found that adding a topical drug to excimer laser or light therapy gave more patients near-complete repigmentation (76% to 100%) than excimer alone, without a significant rise in side effects. Doctors often combine excimer with creams for this reason.

What to Check Before Buying an Excimer Machine

  • Wavelength stated as 308 nm (XeCl)
  • Power density and dose range (mJ/cm²)
  • Spot size: larger spots treat bigger patches faster
  • Lamp life and replacement price
  • Dose memory and patient records
  • Eye protection, training and service in your city
