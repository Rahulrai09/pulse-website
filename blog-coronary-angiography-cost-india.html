Coronary Angiography Cost in India: CT vs Cath Lab, City and CGHS

A coronary angiogram shows whether the heart arteries are blocked. This guide shows what a CT and a cath lab angiogram cost in Indian cities, what CGHS pays, and what adds to the bill.

PBy Pulse Team · Updated November 14, 2026 · 4 min read

A coronary angiography in India costs about Rs 15,000 to Rs 60,000 in a cath lab and about Rs 9,000 to Rs 15,000 as a CT angiogram, according to a 2026 insurer cost guide. Government hospitals charge less, and CGHS pays Rs 13,400 for a coronary angiography at NABH hospitals in metro cities.

Government Hospitals and CGHS

The same 2026 guide puts a cath lab angiogram at about Rs 8,000 to Rs 18,000 in a government hospital general ward, and says it is free for eligible PM-JAY (Ayushman Bharat) patients, subject to scheme rules.

CT Angiography vs Cath Lab Angiography

  • CT angiography: dye is given through a vein in the arm and a CT scanner takes pictures. No hospital stay. It is often used to rule out disease.
  • Cath lab angiography: a thin tube is passed from the wrist or groin to the heart and dye is injected directly into the arteries. It gives the clearest pictures, and a blockage can be treated in the same sitting.

Your cardiologist decides which test you need.

What Adds to the Bill

  • Room type for the observation period.
  • Contrast dye: some dyes used for patients with weak kidneys cost more.
  • Tests before the procedure: kidney and clotting blood tests, an ECG and sometimes a chest X-ray. See our ECG test price and LFT and KFT test price guides.
  • Doctor's fees and the hospital's category.

What a Cath Lab Uses for an Angiogram

A cath lab angiogram uses a set of single-use items: an introducer sheath or micro-access kit to enter the artery, a guidewire, a diagnostic catheter, a manifold, high pressure and pressure monitoring lines, and contrast dye. Angiography Cost: FAQs

What is the cost of coronary angiography in India?

A cath lab coronary angiography costs about Rs 15,000 to Rs 60,000 in 2026, according to an insurer's cost guide. A CT coronary angiography costs about Rs 9,000 to Rs 15,000.

What is the CGHS rate for coronary angiography?

From 13 October 2025, CGHS pays Rs 13,400 at NABH hospitals in metro cities, Rs 12,060 in Tier Y cities and Rs 10,720 in Tier Z cities. Non-NABH hospitals are paid 15% less.

Is angiography free under Ayushman Bharat?

An insurer's 2026 cost guide says it is free for eligible PM-JAY patients at empanelled hospitals, subject to scheme rules. Check with the hospital before admission.

What is the difference between CT angiography and normal angiography?

CT angiography uses a scanner and dye through an arm vein, with no hospital stay. Cath lab angiography passes a thin tube to the heart and gives clearer pictures, and a blockage can be treated at once.

Does health insurance cover angiography?

Most health plans cover angiography when a doctor advises it, but room-rent caps, sub-limits and waiting periods for heart conditions may apply.
