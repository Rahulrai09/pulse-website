Which Type Do You Need?

  • Self-propelled: large rear wheels with hand rims, so the user can push the chair alone.
  • Transit: small wheels, pushed by a carer. Lighter and narrower, so it fits lifts and doorways.
  • Steel or aluminium: steel costs less and is strong, but heavy. Aluminium is easier to lift into a car.
  • Commode: has a seat with an opening for toilet use, which helps with care at home.
  • Reclining: the back leans far back, for people who tire sitting up or need to rest during the day.

What the Indian Standard Asks For

The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) standard for adult folding wheelchairs is IS 7454:2024. A chair made to it, and BIS certified, can carry the ISI mark. Key points:

ItemIS 7454:2024
Weight of a basic chair25 kg at most
Seat height at the front480 to 510 mm
Seat depth420 to 440 mm
Width open / folded650 to 720 mm / 300 to 330 mm
Rear wheels24 inch
Tests100 kg load tests, 250 folding cycles and a stability test on a 9° slope

For hospitals and government tenders, asking for the ISI mark under IS 7454 is a simple way to check quality. The ALIMCO standard folding chair, for example, is ISI marked.

What to Check Before You Buy

  • User weight: many basic chairs are rated for about 100 kg. Choose a heavy-duty chair for heavier users.
  • Seat width: the user should sit with a little space on each side.
  • Brakes: both rear brakes should lock firmly.
  • Footrests and armrests: swing-away footrests and lift-up armrests make moving to a bed easier.
  • Tyres: solid tyres need no air; air tyres ride softer outdoors.
  • Folded size and weight: check that it fits your car boot and that the carer can lift it.
  • Cleaning: wipe-clean seats suit hospitals and commode use.

Free or Subsidised Wheelchairs

If the user has a disability certificate of 40% or more and family income up to Rs 30,000 a month, the government ADIP scheme can give a wheelchair free or at half cost. See our ADIP scheme guide.

Manual Wheelchair Price: FAQs

What is the price of a manual wheelchair in India?

Online medical stores list manual wheelchairs at about Rs 4,300 to Rs 18,000 in November 2026. Basic folding steel chairs start near Rs 4,300, aluminium chairs cost about Rs 6,800 to Rs 9,600 and reclining chairs about Rs 11,800.

Which is better, a steel or an aluminium wheelchair?

Steel chairs cost less and are strong but heavy. Aluminium chairs cost a little more and are easier to lift into a car, which helps carers and travel.

What is a transit wheelchair?

A transit wheelchair has small rear wheels and is pushed by a carer. It is lighter and narrower than a self-propelled chair, so it fits lifts and doorways easily.

What is the Indian standard for wheelchairs?

IS 7454:2024 covers adult folding wheelchairs. It sets sizes, a 25 kg maximum weight for a basic chair and tests such as 100 kg load tests and 250 folding cycles. Certified chairs can carry the ISI mark.

Can I get a free wheelchair in India?

Yes, if you qualify for the government ADIP scheme: a disability certificate of 40% or more and family income up to Rs 30,000 a month. Lower incomes get the chair free; others get half the cost.
