Medical Equipment Price List in India 2026: Ventilators, Dialysis Machines, Stents and More

Buying medical equipment in India is hard when nobody shows a price. This guide puts the price ranges for common hospital and clinic equipment in one simple table, with real government tender prices, the costs people forget, and how to get a fair quote.

Quick answer: In India in 2026, an ICU ventilator usually costs about Rs 7 lakh to Rs 22 lakh, a new haemodialysis machine about Rs 5 lakh to Rs 15 lakh, an electric wheelchair about Rs 35,000 to Rs 2.5 lakh, and a CO2 fractional laser about Rs 2.5 lakh to Rs 12.5 lakh. Heart stents have a government price cap: at most Rs 39,186.03 for a drug-eluting stent, plus GST. Prices change with features, brand, service and taxes.

Why Prices Vary So Much

  • Features: more modes, better monitoring and longer battery backup cost more.
  • Imported or made in India: imported machines usually cost more and can take longer to service.
  • Service and warranty: a longer warranty, a nearby engineer and spare parts in India add to the price but save money later.
  • Consumables: some machines need parts you buy again and again. A cheap machine with costly consumables can cost more over time.
  • Taxes and delivery: many prices are shown before GST, delivery and installation. Always ask.

Costs People Forget

  1. GST: check the rate for each item. For example, wheelchairs carry 5% GST.
  2. Installation and training: is it free or extra?
  3. Consumables: dialysers, tubing, filters, tips and other parts used every day.
  4. Service contract (AMC): what it costs each year after the warranty ends.
  5. Room and utilities: a dialysis unit needs a water (RO) plant; an ICU needs oxygen and power backup.
  6. Spare parts and batteries: for example, a lithium wheelchair battery usually lasts about 3 to 5 years.
The lowest price is not always the lowest cost. Compare the total cost over five years, not just the price on day one.

How to Get a Fair Quote: 5 Steps

  1. Write down exactly what you need: model type, features and quantity.
  2. Ask at least three sellers for a written, itemised quote.
  3. Check that each quote shows GST, delivery, installation, training and warranty.
  4. Ask for the price of consumables and the yearly service contract.
  5. Ask for a demo and the regulatory papers (such as CDSCO registration) before you pay.

Medical Equipment Price List in India 2026: FAQs

How much does an ICU ventilator cost in India?

Published market guides put ICU ventilators at about Rs 7 lakh to Rs 22 lakh or more, before GST. Transport ventilators cost about Rs 1.5 lakh to Rs 5.5 lakh, and BiPAP units about Rs 1 lakh to Rs 3 lakh.

How much does a dialysis machine cost in India?

A new haemodialysis machine usually costs about Rs 5 lakh to Rs 15 lakh. High-end imported machines can cost Rs 15 lakh to Rs 25 lakh or more, and refurbished machines about Rs 2 lakh to Rs 5 lakh.

What is the maximum price of a stent in India?

From 1 April 2026, the NPPA ceiling price is Rs 39,186.03 for a drug-eluting stent and Rs 10,762.15 for a bare metal stent, plus GST. The cap covers the stent only, not the full hospital bill.

Are these prices including GST?

Usually not. Most published ranges and seller listings are before GST, delivery and installation. Always ask for an itemised quote that shows every cost.
