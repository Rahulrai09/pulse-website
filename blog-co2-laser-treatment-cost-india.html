CO2 Laser Treatment Cost in India: Acne Scars and Skin Resurfacing

Fractional CO2 laser is one of the most effective treatments for acne scars. This guide shows what a session costs in India, how many sessions people need, the downtime, and how it compares with other skin lasers.

PBy Pulse Team · Updated November 14, 2026 · 4 min read
Share

A fractional CO2 laser session for acne scars in India costs about Rs 8,500 to Rs 25,000, based on 2026 hospital and insurer guides. Most people need 3 to 6 sessions spaced weeks apart, so a full course often costs about Rs 25,000 to Rs 1 lakh or more. Expect 5 to 14 days of redness and peeling each time.

CO2 Laser Cost per Session

Published 2026 guides give these per-session ranges for fractional CO2 laser in India:

SourceCO2 laser per sessionNote
Hospital guide, Delhi NCR (June 2026)Rs 8,500 – 18,000Fractional CO2, 5 to 7 days downtime
Insurer guide, MumbaiRs 8,000 – 25,000CO2 laser for acne scars
Finance guide (November 2026)Rs 10,000 – 50,000Scar removal or skin resurfacing, per treatment

Metro and dermatologist-led clinics sit at the top of these ranges. A very cheap "CO2" session may not use a genuine fractional CO2 machine, so ask which machine the clinic uses.

How Many Sessions, and the Total Cost

  • Mild scars: 1 to 3 sessions.
  • Moderate scars: about 3 to 6 sessions. One hospital guide expects about 50% to 70% improvement after 4 to 6 sessions.
  • Deep or pitted scars: 6 or more sessions, often combined with other treatments. Complete removal is not expected.

Sessions are usually spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart, and skin keeps improving for 3 to 6 months after the last one. At Rs 10,000 to Rs 18,000 a session, 4 to 6 sessions cost about Rs 40,000 to Rs 1.08 lakh (our estimate). Packages often lower the price per session.

Downtime and Safety

  • Redness, swelling and peeling are expected for about 5 to 14 days.
  • New skin usually covers the area in 7 to 10 days, but full healing takes at least a month.
  • Brown skin has a higher risk of colour changes after CO2 laser, so careful settings, a patch test and sun protection matter.
  • Choose a qualified dermatologist; the person and the settings matter as much as the machine.

Does Insurance Cover CO2 Laser?

Usually not when it is done for cosmetic reasons such as acne scars or wrinkles. Check with your insurer if a doctor advises it for a medical reason.

CO2 Laser Treatment Cost: FAQs

What is the cost of CO2 laser treatment in India?

About Rs 8,500 to Rs 25,000 a session for acne scars, based on 2026 hospital and insurer guides. Larger resurfacing treatments can cost more.

How many CO2 laser sessions are needed for acne scars?

Most people need 3 to 6 sessions. Mild scars may need 1 to 3, and deep scars 6 or more, often with other treatments.

What is the downtime after CO2 laser?

Redness, swelling and peeling usually last about 5 to 14 days. Full healing takes at least a month.

Is CO2 laser safe for Indian skin?

It can be used, but brown skin has a higher risk of colour changes. A qualified dermatologist, careful settings, a patch test and sun protection are important.

Is RF microneedling better than CO2 laser?

RF microneedling is gentler with less downtime and is often chosen for darker skin. CO2 works deeper. A dermatologist should choose based on your scars and skin.
