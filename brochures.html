Using a brochure

How to compare equipment brochures

Quick answer: Use a brochure to shortlist models, then check the points that change the price and the fit: the exact model, key specifications, certifications, what comes in the box and the service plan. Ask the supplier to confirm anything that matters in writing.
  • Match the model name. Brochures often cover several versions. Note the exact model before you ask for a price.
  • Check the specifications you need. For example, screen size and modes on a ventilator, channels on an ECG, or test speed on an analyzer.
  • Check certifications. Ask for the CDSCO licence and ISO 13485 certificate for the product you are buying.
  • Ask what is included. Accessories, consumables, installation and training are not always part of the base price.
  • Ask about service. Warranty, AMC options and how quickly an engineer can reach your site.

Ready to price it? Request a quote with the model name from the brochure, or see where to buy.
