RF Microneedling (MNRF) Cost in India: Price per Session

RF microneedling, also called MNRF, treats acne scars, open pores and loose skin with tiny needles and radiofrequency heat. This guide shows what a session costs in India, how many sessions you need and how it compares with CO2 laser.

PBy Pulse Team · Updated November 14, 2026 · 4 min read
Share

RF microneedling (MNRF) in India costs about Rs 12,000 to Rs 25,000 a session at many clinics, and up to about Rs 40,000 at premium dermatology clinics, based on 2026 hospital and dermatologist guides. Most people need 3 to 4 sessions, spaced 4 to 8 weeks apart, with 2 to 3 days of downtime.

Plain microneedling without RF costs less. A health platform lists microneedling in India at about Rs 2,000 to Rs 35,000 a session, depending on the city and device. Ask whether your quote is for RF microneedling or plain microneedling.

How Many Sessions, and the Total Cost

Most people need 3 to 4 sessions, spaced 4 to 8 weeks apart. Results build over 2 to 3 months as new collagen forms. At Rs 15,000 a session, 3 to 4 sessions cost about Rs 45,000 to Rs 60,000 (our estimate). Packages often lower the price per session.

What RF Microneedling Treats

  • Acne scars, especially rolling and boxcar scars.
  • Open pores and uneven texture.
  • Mild sagging on the face, jawline and neck.
  • Fine lines and early signs of ageing.

It suits early to moderate skin laxity. Heavy, loose skin usually needs surgery.

Because the energy goes in through the needles rather than as light on the surface, RF microneedling carries less risk of dark patches on brown skin. See our CO2 laser treatment cost guide for the laser option.

Downtime and Aftercare

  • Numbing cream is applied first, so most people feel only mild discomfort.
  • Redness and small marks usually settle in 2 to 3 days.
  • Use sunscreen and avoid harsh skin products for a few days, as your doctor advises.

Insurance usually does not cover it, as it is treated as cosmetic.

For Clinics: What a Session Has to Pay For

Medical-grade RF microneedling machines cost about Rs 8 lakh to Rs 25 lakh in India (see our RF microneedling machine price guide). As a rough example (our estimate), a Rs 10 lakh machine needs about 67 sessions at Rs 15,000 to cover its price, before needle tips, staff and rent. Disposable needle tips are a running cost, so ask for the price per tip.

RF Microneedling Cost: FAQs

What is the cost of RF microneedling in India?

About Rs 12,000 to Rs 25,000 a session at many clinics, and up to about Rs 40,000 at premium dermatology clinics, based on 2026 guides.

How many MNRF sessions are needed?

Most people need 3 to 4 sessions, spaced 4 to 8 weeks apart. Results build over 2 to 3 months.

What is the downtime after RF microneedling?

Usually 2 to 3 days of redness and small marks. Most people return to work quickly.

Is RF microneedling better than CO2 laser for acne scars?

RF microneedling has less downtime and lower risk of dark patches on brown skin. CO2 laser works more aggressively on the surface. A dermatologist should choose based on your scars.

Is MNRF the same as microneedling?

No. MNRF adds radiofrequency heat through the needles. Plain microneedling costs less but does not heat the deeper skin.
