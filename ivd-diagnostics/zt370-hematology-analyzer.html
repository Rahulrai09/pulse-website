Technical Specifications

Parameters WBC, LYM#, MID#, GRAN#, LYM%, MID%, GRAN%, RBC, HGB, HCT, MCV, MCH, MCHC, RDW-CV, RDW-SD, PLT, MPV, PDW-SD, PDW-CV, PCT, P-LCR, P-LCC — 3-part differentiation of WBC, 22 parameters and 3 histograms
Principle of Operation WBC/RBC/PLT: electrical impedance; HGB: photoelectric colorimetry
Aspiration Volume Whole blood 8.5µL, pre-diluted 20µL
Aperture Diameter WBC 100µm, RBC/PLT 68µm
Throughput 72 tests/hour
Data Storage Up to 1,000,000 sample results with histograms
Dilution Ratio WBC/HGB — 1:232 (whole blood), 1:400 (capillary); RBC/PLT — 1:40000 (whole blood), 1:45000 (capillary)
Display 10.4-inch colour LCD, resolution 800×600, touchscreen
Control & Calibration L-J, X, X-R, X-B four control modes, manual and auto-calibration
Input / Output RS232, 6× USB (mouse, keyboard, HP printer, barcode reader, flash disk), internal 57mm thermal printer
Power AC 100–240V, 50/60Hz, 180VA
Dimensions & Weight 380mm (L) × 305mm (W) × 395mm (H), about 18kg
