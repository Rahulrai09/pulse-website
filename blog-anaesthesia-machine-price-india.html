Anaesthesia Machine and Workstation Price in India

Anaesthesia machines in India range from about Rs 3 lakh to over Rs 40 lakh. This guide explains the price bands, how a workstation differs from a basic machine, and what to check before you buy for your OT.

How much does an anaesthesia machine cost in India? Bajaj Finserv's September 2026 guide puts basic anaesthesia machines at about Rs 3 lakh to Rs 6 lakh, mid-range workstations at about Rs 8 lakh to Rs 15 lakh, and advanced workstations at about Rs 18 lakh to Rs 40 lakh or more. The ventilator, monitoring, vaporizers and service terms decide the price.

Machine vs Workstation: What You Get

  • Anaesthesia machine: delivers oxygen, air or nitrous oxide and anaesthetic vapour through flowmeters and vaporizers, with a manual breathing bag.
  • Anaesthesia workstation: adds an integrated ventilator, a screen with waveforms, built-in monitoring of oxygen, carbon dioxide and anaesthetic agents, and electronic alarms and self-checks.

What Drives the Price

1. Ventilator

The range of modes, tidal volume range (adult and paediatric, or neonatal too), and pneumatic or electronic drive make the biggest difference.

2. Gas monitoring

FiO2, EtCO2 and anaesthetic agent monitoring (AGM, MAC) add cost and improve safety.

3. Vaporizers and flowmeters

Two vaporizer mounts with an interlock, and precise flowmeters suitable for low-flow anaesthesia, are standard on good workstations.

4. Safety systems

Oxygen failure alarms, hypoxic guard, APL valve, battery backup and an anaesthetic gas scavenging system (AGSS) protect patients and OT staff.

5. Service and calibration

Workstations need regular calibration and sensor replacement. Fast local service keeps OTs running.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an anaesthesia machine cost in India?

Bajaj Finserv's September 2026 guide puts basic anaesthesia machines at about Rs 3 lakh to Rs 6 lakh, mid-range workstations at about Rs 8 lakh to Rs 15 lakh, and advanced workstations at about Rs 18 lakh to Rs 40 lakh or more.

What is the difference between an anaesthesia machine and an anaesthesia workstation?

A basic anaesthesia machine delivers oxygen, medical gases and anaesthetic vapour. A workstation adds an integrated ventilator, built-in monitoring such as FiO2, EtCO2 and anaesthetic gas levels, and electronic safety checks and alarms.

What should I check before buying an anaesthesia workstation?

Check the ventilator modes and tidal volume range, number of vaporizer mounts and interlock, flowmeters and low-flow capability, gas monitoring (FiO2, EtCO2, MAC), oxygen failure alarms, battery backup, AGSS option, and local service and calibration support.

Why do government tenders show much higher values?

Large hospital tenders often specify top-end workstations with advanced ventilation, full gas monitoring and long service terms. For example, an SCTIMST tender in September 2026 declared an estimated value of Rs 65 lakh for one high-end workstation, for EMD purposes only.

What are the running costs of an anaesthesia workstation?

Soda lime (CO2 absorbent), breathing circuits and filters, sensors such as oxygen cells and flow sensors, vaporizer servicing and calibration, and an AMC after the warranty. Low-flow anaesthesia reduces volatile agent use.
