GST on Medical Equipment in India: 5% or 18%, with HSN Codes

Since September 2025, most medical devices in India carry 5% GST. Hospital furniture and service contracts still carry 18%. This guide shows which rate applies to what, with HSN codes, examples and tips for buyers.

PBy Pulse Team · Updated November 16, 2026 · 4 min read

Most medical devices, instruments and apparatus in India carry 5% GST since 22 September 2025, down from 12% or 18%. Medical furniture such as hospital beds and operating tables stays at 18%, and maintenance contracts (AMC, CMC, repair) are taxed at 18%. Healthcare services by hospitals and doctors remain exempt.

The same firm notes that non-medical thermometers, lab analysers for non-medical use and industrial X-ray stay at 18%.

What Changed in September 2025?

The GST Council cut rates from 22 September 2025. The government's health summary lists medical devices, test kits, medical oxygen, gauze, bandages, gloves and glucometers moving from 12% to 5%. In July 2026 the finance ministry told Parliament that medical devices now carry 5% unless they are exempt.

Why Hospital Furniture Is Still 18%

Medical furniture falls under HSN 9402, which was not cut. In a ruling reported in October 2026, Gujarat's tax ruling body put motorised surgeon's chairs, eye operating tables and instrument trolleys under 9402 at 18%. The maker had argued they were medical instruments at 5%. So budget 18% for beds, OT tables, delivery tables, stretchers and many trolleys, unless your supplier's HSN says otherwise.

GST on AMC, Rental and Repair

  • AMC, CMC, calibration and repair: 18%, even when the device itself is at 5%. A Rs 40,000 yearly contract carries Rs 7,200 GST.
  • Rental: the same rate as buying the item. Renting a 5% device carries 5%; renting a hospital bed carries 18%.
  • Free warranty repairs: no GST when the customer is not charged.

Can Hospitals Claim Input Tax Credit?

Usually not. Care given by hospitals, clinics and doctors is exempt from GST. So a hospital that only gives exempt care cannot claim back the GST it pays on equipment. The GST on your bill is a real cost, which is why the cut to 5% matters.

GST TDS for Government Hospitals

Government hospitals deduct 2% GST TDS (1% CGST plus 1% SGST) on contracts above Rs 2.5 lakh, not counting GST. The supplier gets credit for it. For example, on a Rs 8 lakh machine at 5% GST, the hospital deducts Rs 16,000 and pays Rs 8,24,000. Most government buying runs through GeM; see our GeM guide.

Tips for Hospital and Clinic Buyers

  • Ask for the HSN code and GST rate on every line of the quote.
  • Compare prices including GST: some sellers show prices before GST.
  • Budget 18% for furniture and service contracts, and 5% for most devices and consumables.
  • If a seller charges 12% or 18% on a medical device, ask why.
  • Buying on a loan? Lenders need the quote with GST; see our equipment loan and EMI guide.

GST on Medical Equipment: Key Facts

  • Most medical devices, instruments and apparatus carry 5% GST in India since 22 September 2025.
  • Hospital beds, operating tables and other medical furniture (HSN 9402) carry 18%.
  • AMC, CMC, calibration and repair services carry 18%, even for 5% devices.
  • Hospitals supplying only exempt healthcare cannot claim input tax credit, so GST is a real cost.
  • Government hospitals deduct 2% GST TDS on contracts above Rs 2.5 lakh.

GST on Medical Equipment: FAQs

What is the GST rate on medical equipment in India?

Most medical devices, instruments and apparatus carry 5% GST since 22 September 2025, unless specifically exempted. Medical furniture such as hospital beds and operating tables carries 18%.

What is the GST on hospital beds?

Hospital beds with mechanical fittings fall under HSN 9402 and carry 18% GST. Renting a hospital bed also carries 18%.

What is the GST on AMC for medical equipment?

18%. Maintenance, calibration and repair are services under SAC 9987, taxed at 18% even when the device itself carries 5%.

Can hospitals claim GST input tax credit on equipment?

Usually not. Healthcare services are exempt, so a hospital that supplies only exempt services cannot claim back the GST it pays on equipment.

What is the GST on wheelchairs and patient monitors?

Both usually carry 5%: wheelchairs under HSN 8713 and electro-medical apparatus such as monitors under HSN 9018.
