Nursing Home Setup Cost in India: 10, 20 and 30 Bed Budgets

A nursing home or small hospital of 10 to 30 beds is the most common way doctors start inpatient care in India. This guide shows what it costs to build and equip one, the licences you need, staffing and monthly running costs.

PBy Pulse Team · Updated November 16, 2026 · 4 min read
Share

A nursing home in India costs about Rs 1.5 crore to Rs 2.5 crore for 10 beds, Rs 3 crore to Rs 5 crore for 20 beds and Rs 5 crore to Rs 8 crore for 30 beds, not counting land, according to a chartered accountant's 2026 project guide. Medical equipment is about a quarter to a third of the budget, and you should keep 3 to 6 months of running costs aside.

A 10-bed home usually has an OPD, 8 to 10 ward beds, a labour or observation bed and a procedure room. A 20-bed home adds an OT, 2 to 3 ICU or recovery beds and basic X-ray, ultrasound and lab. A 30-bed home adds a 4 to 6 bed ICU or HDU and often a second OT.

Licences and Approvals

  • Clinical establishment registration with your state (and a local health authority NOC).
  • Fire safety NOC.
  • Biomedical waste authorisation.
  • Drug licence for the pharmacy, with a qualified pharmacist.
  • Pollution control consent if you run a generator, boiler or incinerator.
  • AERB approval for X-ray equipment.
  • Optional: NABH accreditation and Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY) empanelment.

Licences cost about Rs 2 lakh to Rs 5 lakh for 10 to 30 beds, and can take 6 to 12 months, so start early. See our NABH equipment requirements guide.

Staff and Monthly Running Costs

SizeDoctorsNursesMonthly running cost
10 beds2 – 34 – 6Rs 5 – 10 lakh
20 beds4 – 68 – 12Rs 10 – 20 lakh
30 beds6 – 1012 – 20Rs 15 – 30 lakh

Salaries are the biggest running cost. A common norm is at least one nurse for every 6 ward beds and one per ICU bed, round the clock. Occupancy often starts at 25% to 30% in the first year and grows to 60% to 70% over 3 to 4 years, so plan cash for the slow start.

Ways to Keep Costs Down

  • Rent or lease the building at first instead of buying land.
  • Start with the departments you need, and leave space to add an ICU or second OT later.
  • Buy equipment as one package from a supplier who installs, trains and services it.
  • Weighing used machines? See our refurbished vs new equipment guide.
  • Use a simple, low-cost hospital software at the start.
  • Spread big equipment costs with equipment finance; see our equipment loan and EMI guide.

Nursing Home Setup Cost: FAQs

How much does it cost to start a nursing home in India?

About Rs 1.5 crore to Rs 2.5 crore for 10 beds, Rs 3 crore to Rs 5 crore for 20 beds and Rs 5 crore to Rs 8 crore for 30 beds, not counting land, according to a 2026 chartered accountant project guide.

How much does medical equipment cost for a 20-bed hospital?

About Rs 80 lakh to Rs 1.4 crore, including ward, OT, ICU and diagnostic equipment, according to the same guide. A hospital consultant's guide puts it at Rs 80 lakh to Rs 1.5 crore.

What licences are needed to open a nursing home in India?

Clinical establishment registration, fire safety NOC, biomedical waste authorisation, a drug licence for the pharmacy, pollution control consent if needed, and AERB approval for X-ray. NABH and PMJAY are optional.

How many staff does a 20-bed nursing home need?

About 4 to 6 doctors and 8 to 12 nurses, plus technicians and support staff. A common norm is one nurse per 6 ward beds and one per ICU bed.

What are the monthly running costs of a small hospital?

About Rs 5 lakh to Rs 10 lakh for 10 beds, Rs 10 lakh to Rs 20 lakh for 20 beds and Rs 15 lakh to Rs 30 lakh for 30 beds. Salaries are the biggest part.
