What a Micro-Access Kit Does

Micro-access (micropuncture) starts with a very thin 21G needle and a fine .018" wire instead of a larger needle. The small puncture is easier to place, especially with ultrasound, and causes less harm if the first attempt is not right. Once the wire is in, a dilator and sheath upsize the access to the size needed for the procedure.

The AcuSafe kit puts the micropuncture set and a valved introducer in one pack, with sheaths from 4F to 8F, two lengths and colour-coded hubs. Echogenic needles, which show up better on ultrasound, are available on selected kits.

What to Check Before Ordering Micro-Access Kits

  • Sheath size. Choose 4F to 8F to match the catheters and devices you use.
  • Sheath length. 5.75 cm or 11 cm, depending on the access site.
  • Needle length. 2.5, 5.1 or 7 cm for different depths.
  • Wire type. Stainless steel or nitinol with a palladium tip.
  • Ultrasound. Ask for echogenic needles if you use ultrasound-guided access.
