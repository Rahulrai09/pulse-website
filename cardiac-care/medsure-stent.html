Side-Branch Access With MedSure

When a narrowing sits where a side branch leaves the main artery (a bifurcation), the cardiologist often stents the main branch first, then opens the side branch through the stent struts. MedSure has open cells in its middle segment for this. The struts there can be widened with a semi-compliant or non-compliant balloon such as Glider SC or NC, and a second stent can be passed into the side branch.

What to Check Before Adding a Stent to Your Cath Lab

  • CDSCO licence. Coronary stents are Class D, the highest risk group. Ask for the current licence.
  • Size matrix. Check the diameters and lengths available against your case mix, so you are not short of a size mid-case.
  • Drug and coating. Note the drug (everolimus or sirolimus) and ask for the coating and polymer details.
  • Evidence. Ask for the clinical and bench test data your cardiologists want to review.
  • Price. In India, drug-eluting stent prices are capped by the NPPA. See our stent price guide.
  • Supply and consignment. Agree stock levels, expiry handling and how quickly sizes are replaced.

MedSure Sirolimus Stent FAQs

MedSure is a drug-eluting coronary stent used in angioplasty to hold a narrowed or blocked heart artery open. It releases sirolimus to help stop the artery from narrowing again.

MedSure has open cells in the middle of the stent. A balloon can be passed through them to open a side branch, and a second stent can be placed there if needed.

Diameters of 2.25, 2.50, 2.75, 3.00, 3.50, 4.00 and 4.50 mm, and lengths from 12 mm to 54 mm. The 2.25 mm size goes up to 38 mm long and the 2.50 mm size up to 46 mm.

The nominal pressure is 9 atm and the rated burst pressure is 16 atm. The compliance chart on this page shows the stent diameter at each pressure.

Both release sirolimus. MedSure has thinner 59 µm cobalt chromium struts and open mid-segment cells for side-branch access; Sirova has 65 µm struts and S-link connectors for flexibility.

The MedSure brochure lists ISO 13485:2016 and CE marking (notified body 1035). Ask the Pulse team for the current CDSCO licence and certificates.
