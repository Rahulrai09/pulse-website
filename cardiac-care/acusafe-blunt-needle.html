How the Blunt Needle System Is Used

Percutaneous drainage removes fluid or pus from inside the body through the skin, usually guided by ultrasound or CT. The AcuSafe needle comes with two stylets. The sharp stylet pierces the skin and tissue. Near the target, the operator switches to the blunt stylet, which pushes nearby structures aside instead of cutting them, for more controlled access.

The needles are 18G in 10, 15 and 20 cm lengths. The range also includes AcuSafe introducer sheath needles (19G with a 5F sheath, taking wires up to .038").

What to Check Before Ordering

  • Length. Choose 10, 15 or 20 cm by how deep the target usually is.
  • Guidance. Check the needle shows clearly on your ultrasound or CT.
  • Wire and drain fit. Make sure the needle and sheath fit the guidewires and drains you use next.
  • Pack size. Needles come in boxes of 5; introducer sheath needles in boxes of 10.
