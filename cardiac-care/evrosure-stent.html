How a Drug-Eluting Stent Like EvroSure Is Used

A coronary stent is a small metal mesh tube that holds a narrowed heart artery open. It comes crimped on a balloon. The cardiologist guides it to the blockage over a guidewire, then inflates the balloon with an inflation device to press the stent into the artery wall. The balloon is removed and the stent stays.

EvroSure is coated with everolimus, a drug released slowly into the artery wall to help stop tissue from growing back inside the stent. Its thin 59 µm cobalt chromium struts help it bend through curved arteries, and its polymer coating breaks down completely in 6 to 7 weeks.

What to Check Before Adding a Stent to Your Cath Lab

  • CDSCO licence. Coronary stents are Class D, the highest risk group. Ask for the current licence.
  • Size matrix. Check the diameters and lengths available against your case mix, so you are not short of a size mid-case.
  • Drug and coating. Note the drug (everolimus or sirolimus) and ask for the coating and polymer details.
  • Evidence. Ask for the clinical and bench test data your cardiologists want to review.
  • Price. In India, drug-eluting stent prices are capped by the NPPA. See our stent price guide.
  • Supply and consignment. Agree stock levels, expiry handling and how quickly sizes are replaced.

EvroSure Everolimus Stent FAQs

EvroSure is a drug-eluting coronary stent used in angioplasty to hold a narrowed or blocked heart artery open. It releases everolimus to help stop the artery from narrowing again.

It carries everolimus at 1.1 µg/mm² in a biodegradable PLLA and PLGA polymer. The drug is released in about 4 weeks, and the polymer fully breaks down in 6 to 7 weeks.

The struts are 59 µm thick, on a cobalt chromium L605 platform with a hybrid cell design and non-linear links for flexibility.

Diameters of 2.25, 2.50, 2.75, 3.00, 3.50, 4.00 and 4.50 mm, and lengths from 12 mm to 54 mm. The 2.25 mm size goes up to 38 mm long and the 2.50 mm size up to 46 mm.

The nominal pressure is 8 atm and the rated burst pressure is 16 atm. The compliance chart on this page shows the stent diameter at each pressure.

Evera is also everolimus-eluting and Sirova is sirolimus-eluting, both with 65 µm struts. MedSure is sirolimus-eluting with 59 µm struts and open mid-segment cells. EvroSure pairs everolimus with thin 59 µm cobalt chromium struts and a polymer that fully breaks down in 6 to 7 weeks.

The EvroSure brochure lists ISO 13485:2016 and CE marking (notified body 1035). Ask the Pulse team for the current CDSCO licence and certificates.
