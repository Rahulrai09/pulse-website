What Counts as Rehabilitation Equipment

Rehabilitation equipment helps people move around and manage daily life. In this category, most products are power (electric) wheelchairs. They come in these types:

  • Heavy-duty, all-terrain power wheelchairs
  • Compact foldable electric wheelchairs for indoor/outdoor use
  • Lightweight, easy-transport models
  • Auto-folding wheelchairs designed for travel
  • Reclining power wheelchairs for patients needing extended rest support

The right choice depends on the user. A patient who travels often has different needs from one who needs a heavy-duty chair for daily all-terrain use.

What Actually Differentiates Power Wheelchairs

Most power wheelchairs do the same basic job. The real differences between models come down to a few specs that matter in daily use:

  • Battery range — about 15 km per charge on Pulse models, though this varies with the road and the user's weight
  • Load capacity — 120 kg on most Pulse models and 140 kg on the heavy-duty models
  • Frame weight and portability — lighter aluminium frames (from 19 kg without the battery) matter for anyone who needs to carry the chair by car or flight; steel frames suit heavy-duty use
  • Fold mechanism — standard fold versus one-touch auto-fold changes how practical daily transport actually is
  • Drive configuration — rear-wheel or front-wheel drive options affect manoeuvrability in different settings

What to Verify Before Buying

  • CDSCO class applicable to the device
  • Battery specifications, charging time, and realistic range under load
  • Load capacity and whether it matches the intended user
  • Warranty terms, mainly on the battery and motor
  • Spare-parts availability and service turnaround time
  • Obstacle clearance and suitability for the intended terrain (indoor, outdoor, mixed)
The right power wheelchair depends on how and where it will actually be used — battery range and portability trade-offs that suit a hospital ward may not suit a patient who travels.

Rehabilitation Equipment Manufacturer in India: FAQs

Who manufactures rehabilitation equipment in India?

India's rehabilitation equipment manufacturers include horizontal OEMs, such as Pulse. Pulse makes power wheelchairs and mobility aids for hospitals, clinics, and home rehab. Check each manufacturer's CDSCO licence and certification before you buy.

What rehabilitation equipment is manufactured in India?

Indian manufacturers make power wheelchairs in five types: heavy-duty, foldable, lightweight, auto-folding and reclining. They differ in battery range, load capacity and portability. People use them for rehabilitation and daily mobility.

What should a buyer check before purchasing a power wheelchair from an Indian manufacturer?

Before you buy, check the device's CDSCO classification, battery range, and charging specs. Also check load capacity, frame material, weight, and obstacle clearance. Finally, ask about warranty terms, after-sales service, and spare parts.
