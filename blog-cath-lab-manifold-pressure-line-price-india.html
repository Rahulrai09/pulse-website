What Each Part Does

  • Manifold: a row of taps that joins the catheter to the contrast, the saline flush and the pressure line. Turning the handles switches between them without disconnecting anything.
  • High pressure injection line: carries contrast from a power injector or a syringe to the catheter. It must hold high pressure without bursting or bulging.
  • Pressure monitoring line: links the manifold to the pressure transducer so the team can watch blood pressure inside the heart and vessels.

Pressure Ratings Explained

Pressure is often given in psi (pounds per square inch) or bar (1 bar is about 14.5 psi). Typical ratings from published catalogues are:

ItemTypical rating
Standard manifold500 psi (35 bar); some are made for 1,000 psi
Braided high pressure lineTested up to 1,200 psi (82 bar)
Clear PVC high pressure lineUp to about 1,000 psi
Pressure monitoring lineAbout 560 to 580 psi in the listings above

We suggest a simple rule: every part in the path of a power injection should be rated above the injector's set pressure limit. A monitoring line or a 500 psi manifold should not sit in the path of a high pressure power injection unless its rating covers it.

Braided or Clear PVC?

  • Braided lines have a mesh inside the wall. They hold the highest pressures and resist kinks, so they suit power injectors.
  • Clear PVC lines cost less and let staff see air bubbles easily. They suit hand injections and lower pressures.

What to Check Before You Buy

  • Pressure rating printed on the pack, in psi or bar.
  • Ports and valve type: 2 or 3 ports; "on" or "off" handles to match your team's habit.
  • Length: long enough to reach the injector or transducer without pulling.
  • Connectors: male or female luer lock, and a rotating adapter so the line does not twist.
  • Sterile and single use: sealed packs with a clear expiry date.
  • Licence: ask for the supplier's CDSCO or state licence for each item.

Manifold and Pressure Line Price: FAQs

What is the price of a cath lab manifold in India?

Online listings checked in November 2026 show 2 and 3 port manifolds at about Rs 180 to Rs 900 each.

What is the price of a high pressure line for angiography?

About Rs 160 to Rs 675 each in listings checked in November 2026. One pack of 50 non-braided 100 cm lines is listed at Rs 8,050 plus 12% GST.

What is the difference between a high pressure line and a pressure monitoring line?

A high pressure line carries contrast from an injector to the catheter and is rated up to about 1,200 psi. A pressure monitoring line links the manifold to the pressure transducer and is usually rated much lower.

What pressure rating should a manifold have?

Most cath lab manifolds are rated to 500 psi, and some to 1,000 psi. We suggest that every part in the path of a power injection is rated above the injector's pressure limit.

Should I choose braided or clear PVC lines?

Braided lines hold higher pressure and resist kinks, so they suit power injectors. Clear PVC lines cost less and show air bubbles easily, so they suit hand injections.
