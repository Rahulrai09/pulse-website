Pulse Zuvio Inaugurates Critical Care Manufacturing Unit

Pulse Zuvio Pvt Ltd opened officially at the Andhra Pradesh MedTech Zone (AMTZ) in Visakhapatnam, during World Health Day on April 8. This is a big step for India's push to make critical care equipment at home.

PBy Pulse Team · Updated Article · 3 min read
Share

Dr. Harsh Vardhan, former Union Minister of Health and Family Welfare, led the opening. Dr. Jitendra Sharma, Managing Director and Founder CEO of AMTZ, joined him. The event was part of a bigger push to make India a global hub for medical technology. On the same day, 25 startups signed Letters of Intent (LoIs) for incubation at AMTZ.

What the new unit will build

The Pulse Zuvio facility will make critical care devices built for Indian hospitals, including:

  • Patient monitors
  • Syringe pumps
  • Dialysis machines

India has long depended on imports for these categories, despite strong demand at home. The AMTZ unit gives Pulse Zuvio direct access to the country's first integrated medical-device manufacturing ecosystem. This includes shared facilities for component testing, EMI labs, sterilisation, and regulatory readiness.

Why AMTZ matters

AMTZ is a 270-acre medical-device manufacturing zone, set up by the Government of Andhra Pradesh. It houses costly, shared facilities under one roof, which cuts manufacturing costs for individual companies by 40 to 50 percent. This helps Indian manufacturers compete with global companies on both price and quality.

Speaking at the event, Dr. Harsh Vardhan talked about India's shift from relying on imports to building its own capability in medical technology. He said the AMTZ ecosystem is not just making devices, but "building strategic confidence" for the nation.

From enabling 100% indigenous hospitals under the 'Make in India Hospital' initiative to advancing inclusive innovation through dedicated MedTech venture ecosystems, AMTZ's focus remains clear: to solve critical healthcare challenges with scalable, homegrown solutions. — Dr. Jitendra Sharma, MD & Founder CEO, AMTZ

Where this fits for Pulse

The Zuvio opening follows Pulse's $4 million seed round, led by 3one4 Capital earlier this year. It is a real step in turning that funding into an actual manufacturing footprint. With AMTZ as a base, Pulse Zuvio extends Pulse's full model of design, sourcing, manufacturing, and service into a category where India has long needed more domestic supply.

The same day, AMTZ also laid the foundation stone for Truevis Technologies, which focuses on linear actuators and advanced cancer care. This adds further strength to AMTZ's critical-care manufacturing cluster.
