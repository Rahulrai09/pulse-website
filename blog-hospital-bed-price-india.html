Types of Hospital Beds

A semi Fowler bed raises only the back-rest. A full Fowler bed raises the back-rest and the knee-rest, so the patient does not slide down. ICU beds add height adjustment (hi-low) and head-down and head-up tilts (Trendelenburg and reverse Trendelenburg), usually with stronger side rails and a CPR release. Each type comes in manual (hand crank) and electric versions.

What Drives the Price

1. Number of functions

Each extra position adds mechanics or motors, so a 5-function ICU bed costs far more than a semi Fowler bed.

2. Manual or electric

Electric beds save nursing time and suit frequent position changes. Manual beds cost less and work without power.

3. Frame, panels and rails

Steel frames, ABS head and foot panels and side rail type change both price and how easy the bed is to clean.

4. Castors and extras

Larger locking castors, IV rods, CPR release, separate patient and staff remotes, and the mattress all add cost.

5. Quantity and service

Hospitals ordering beds for whole wards usually get better unit prices. Check the warranty on motors and actuators.

Hospital Bed Price in India: FAQs

How much does a hospital bed cost in India?

Bajaj Finserv's June 2026 guide puts manual semi Fowler beds at about Rs 5,000 to Rs 15,000, electric semi Fowler beds at Rs 20,000 to Rs 60,000, electric full Fowler beds at Rs 30,000 to Rs 60,000 and fully electric ICU beds at Rs 80,000 to Rs 2.5 lakh or more.

What is the difference between a Fowler bed and a semi Fowler bed?

A semi Fowler bed raises only the back-rest. A full Fowler bed raises the back-rest and the knee-rest, so the patient does not slide down when sitting up.

What is a 5-function ICU bed?

A bed with back-rest, knee-rest, height adjustment (hi-low), Trendelenburg (head-down tilt) and reverse Trendelenburg (head-up tilt), used in ICUs and HDUs.

Should a hospital buy manual or electric beds?

Electric beds save nursing time and suit ICUs and frequent position changes. Manual beds cost less and keep working without power, which helps where power is unreliable.

What should a hospital check before buying beds?

Check the functions each ward needs, manual or electric operation, side rails, castors and brakes, mattress size, safe working load, warranty on motors and local service.
