Cath Lab Setup Cost in India: Machine, Room and Consumables

Planning a cath lab? This guide covers the three budgets: the imaging machine, the shielded room around it, and the consumables used in every angioplasty.

PBy Pulse Team · Updated October 31, 2026 · 8 min read
Share

A complete cath lab in India costs about Rs 1.75 crore to Rs 3 crore with a refurbished single-plane machine and Rs 3.5 crore to Rs 5.5 crore with a new one, based on an August 2026 Indian cost breakdown. The machine is about two-thirds of that; the shielded room, air conditioning and power make up the rest. Each angioplasty then uses about Rs 45,000 to Rs 66,000 of consumables.

AERB Approvals You Need

  • Type-approved machine. Buy only AERB type-approved equipment from an authorised supplier.
  • Layout approval. Get the shielding plan approved before building the room.
  • Radiation Safety Officer. Appoint a certified RSO. A missing RSO is a common reason a finished lab cannot get its licence.
  • Licence for operation. Apply on AERB’s eLORA portal after quality tests and a radiation survey. No patient can be treated before it is issued.
  • Staff safety. Lead aprons, protective barriers, radiation badges (TLD) for every worker, and a warning sign on the door.

What a Cath Lab Earns

Under CGHS rates effective October 2025, a PTCA package pays about Rs 71,166 (non-NABH) to Rs 96,284 (super speciality) without a closure device, with the stent paid separately at its NPPA ceiling price. Private hospital angioplasty bills are usually higher. Case volume, consumable prices and staff costs decide how fast a lab pays back.

How to Plan and Save

  • Start with a single-plane lab unless you plan heavy paediatric or structural heart work.
  • A certified refurbished machine can cut the machine cost by half; insist on AERB type approval and a service contract.
  • Get the shielding layout approved before civil work starts, to avoid costly rework.
  • Agree consumable rates and consignment stock with one supplier for stents, balloons, wires and kits.

Cath Lab Setup Cost in India: FAQs

How much does it cost to set up a cath lab in India?

A single-plane cath lab costs about Rs 1.75 crore to Rs 3 crore with a refurbished machine and Rs 3.5 crore to Rs 5.5 crore with a new one, including the room. A biplane lab costs about Rs 8 crore to Rs 12 crore (RayMedico, August 2026).

How much does a cath lab machine cost?

A new single-plane cath lab machine costs about Rs 2.5 crore to Rs 4.5 crore, a refurbished one about Rs 80 lakh to Rs 1.6 crore, and a biplane system about Rs 6 crore to Rs 10 crore. Software for FFR, IVUS and OCT can add Rs 40 lakh to Rs 1 crore.

How much space does a cath lab need?

A single-plane procedure room is usually 40 to 55 sq m, with a control room and a technical room of 12 to 20 sq m each, plus 4 to 8 recovery bays. AERB sets a legal minimum of about 25 sq m, but that is a floor, not a design target.

What approvals does a cath lab need in India?

The X-ray machine must be AERB type-approved, the room shielding layout approved, a Radiation Safety Officer appointed, and a licence for operation obtained on AERB’s eLORA portal before treating patients.

What consumables are used in one angioplasty?

A typical single-stent angioplasty uses an introducer sheath, guiding catheter, guidewire, one or two PTCA balloons, an inflation device, a manifold and a drug-eluting stent. These cost about Rs 45,000 to Rs 66,000 before the guidewire and guiding catheter.
