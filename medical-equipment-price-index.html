Medical Equipment Price Index India 2026

One table of market prices for hospital and clinic equipment in India. Every price has a named source and month, and links to a guide that explains what drives it. Free to use with a link.

Medical equipment prices in India vary widely. In 2026 an ICU ventilator costs about Rs 7 lakh to Rs 22 lakh, a new dialysis machine Rs 5 lakh to Rs 15 lakh, a 5-function electric ICU bed Rs 60,000 to Rs 90,000 and a 3-part hematology analyzer Rs 1.7 lakh to Rs 4 lakh. This index lists 78 prices, each with its source and month.

Price Index: Key Facts

  • 78 price ranges in 9 groups: critical care, renal care, cardiac care, lab and IVD, hospital setup, operation theatre, aesthetics, surgical and mobility.
  • Sources are public: marketplaces (Medikabazaar, IndiaMART, TradeIndia), price guides, GeM tenders, CGHS rates and NPPA orders.
  • The government caps a drug-eluting stent at Rs 39,186.03 plus GST (NPPA, from 1 April 2026).
  • A 10-bed ICU needs about Rs 70 lakh to Rs 2 crore of core equipment, by our estimate.
  • The index is updated every month and is free to use under CC BY 4.0.

How We Build the Index

  • Sources: we only use prices we can open and date: online marketplaces, published price guides, GeM tenders, CGHS rate lists and NPPA orders.
  • Ranges, not single prices: each row covers the usual low and high for that type, so one unusual listing does not skew it.
  • Full sources in the guides: every row links to a Pulse guide that names and links each source.
  • Monthly updates: we refresh the rows each month and drop sources older than 12 months.
  • Not Pulse prices: these are market ranges. Pulse prices are on quote, for small clinics and large hospitals alike.

Price Index FAQs

What is the Pulse Medical Equipment Price Index?

It is a free table of 78 market price ranges for medical equipment and consumables in India, in 9 groups, each with a named source and month. Pulse builds it from its buying guides and updates it every month.

How much does an ICU ventilator cost in India in 2026?

An ICU ventilator usually costs about Rs 7 lakh to Rs 22 lakh or more before GST (Bajaj Finserv, 2026). Transport ventilators cost about Rs 1.5 lakh to Rs 5.5 lakh and BiPAP machines about Rs 1 lakh to Rs 3 lakh.

Are these Pulse prices, and do they include GST?

No. They are market ranges from public listings, price guides and government notices, not Pulse prices, which are on quote. Some sources include GST and some do not, so always ask for a written quote that shows GST.

How often is the price index updated?

Every month. Each row shows the month of its source, and the CSV file carries the date the index was last updated. The next update is due in early December 2026.
