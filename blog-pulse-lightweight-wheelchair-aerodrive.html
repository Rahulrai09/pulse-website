Pulse Lightweight Power Wheelchair: Aerodrive-1 & Aerodrive-2

Between a heavy-duty all-terrain chair and an ultra-light travel model sits a genuinely useful middle ground: a wheelchair light enough to handle daily without a struggle, but capable enough for real outdoor use. That's the Aerodrive range.

What is the Pulse Aerodrive wheelchair? The Pulse Aerodrive-1 and Aerodrive-2 are lightweight power wheelchairs for everyday indoor and outdoor mobility. Aerodrive-2 weighs 36 kg and runs on a 24V 10Ah battery for a 15–20 km range, while Aerodrive-1 adds a premium aluminium frame with dual motors and a smart joystick.

Who It's For

Aerodrive suits users who:

  • Need daily mobility that's easier to lift, load, and manoeuvre than a heavy-duty chair
  • Have a caregiver who regularly helps load or move the wheelchair
  • Use the chair across mixed indoor and outdoor settings, not just flat indoor floors
  • Want dependable daily performance without the bulk of an all-terrain model

How to Use It

Both Aerodrive models are controlled via joystick, with Aerodrive-1's smart joystick designed for smooth navigation in clinical and community settings. Aerodrive-2's 24V 10Ah battery is built for everyday use across a 15–20 km range per charge, with the lighter 36 kg frame making transport between home, vehicle, and destination more manageable for a single caregiver.

Why Choose Aerodrive Over a Heavy-Duty Model

If your daily routine involves loading the wheelchair into a car boot, navigating tight indoor spaces, or a caregiver handling the chair regularly, the weight difference between Aerodrive and a heavy-duty model like Motion Pro adds up meaningfully over repeated daily use. Aerodrive keeps enough capability for outdoor use while staying genuinely manageable day to day.
