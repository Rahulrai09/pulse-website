Hospital Equipment List by Department, with Prices in India

Planning a new hospital or adding a department? This list covers the core equipment for each department, with typical Indian price ranges and a detailed buying guide for every major item.

What equipment does a new hospital need? Each department has its own core list. An ICU needs ventilators, multipara monitors, syringe and infusion pumps, ICU beds, an ECG machine and a crash cart. An OT needs an OT table, OT lights, an anaesthesia workstation, instrument trolleys and sterilisation. A dialysis unit needs haemodialysis machines, an RO water plant and dialysers. Labour rooms need delivery tables and baby warmers, and the lab needs hematology and biochemistry analyzers. How to Use This List

  1. Start from your services and bed count. List the departments you will open in phase one.
  2. Count per bed or per room. ICU items are counted per bed; OT items per theatre; dialysis items per machine.
  3. Add running costs. Consumables, accessories and maintenance often matter more over five years than the purchase price.
  4. Check approvals and service. Confirm licences, installation needs (power, gas, water) and local service before ordering.

Hospital Equipment List: FAQs

What equipment is needed to start a hospital in India?

It depends on the departments you open. Most new hospitals need ICU equipment (ventilators, patient monitors, syringe and infusion pumps, ICU beds, ECG machine, crash cart), OT equipment (OT table, OT lights, anaesthesia workstation, instrument trolleys, autoclave), ward beds and trolleys, and a basic lab with a hematology analyzer. Dialysis, labour room and newborn care add their own lists.

What equipment is needed for an ICU?

For each ICU bed: an ICU bed, a multipara monitor, syringe and infusion pumps, and access to a ventilator. For the unit: an ECG machine, a crash cart with defibrillator, suction, and central monitoring if needed.

What equipment is needed for an operation theatre?

An OT table, a ceiling OT light (often double dome), an anaesthesia workstation, a patient monitor, instrument trolleys, suction, and sterilisation through an autoclave or CSSD. Modular OTs add HVAC, wall panels and medical gas pipelines.

How much does hospital equipment cost in India?

It varies widely by department and size. As examples, ICU ventilators cost about Rs 7 lakh to Rs 22 lakh or more each, multipara monitors about Rs 19,000 to Rs 2.2 lakh, OT tables about Rs 30,000 to Rs 7 lakh, and core equipment for a 4-machine dialysis unit about Rs 37 lakh to Rs 49 lakh.
