Cryolipolysis Machine Price in India: What Clinics Pay in 2026

Cryolipolysis (fat freezing) is one of the most asked-for body treatments in Indian clinics. This guide shows what the machines cost, why prices differ so much, and what to check before you buy.

What Is a Cryolipolysis Machine?

A cryolipolysis machine cools a pinched area of fat through a gel pad and a vacuum handle. Fat cells are more sensitive to cold than skin, so some of them die over the next weeks and the body clears them. No cuts or needles are used.

A 2015 review in Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery found average fat-layer reductions of about 14.7% to 28.5% when measured with callipers, and 10.3% to 25.5% by ultrasound. Two sessions on the same area gave more reduction than one (28.5% vs 19.7% in one study).

What Changes the Price of a Cryolipolysis Machine?

  • Number of handles. More handles let you treat two areas at once, so each session takes less staff time.
  • Handle shapes and sizes. Curved, flat, small (chin) and large (belly) handles let you treat more body areas.
  • Lowest temperature and control. Look for a stable, set temperature with sensors that stop cooling if the skin gets too cold.
  • 360-degree cooling. Handles that cool all around the tissue cover more fat per session and cost more.
  • Brand, papers and service. Imported branded systems with regulatory papers and local engineers sit at the top of the range.

Cryolipolysis Machine Running Costs

The main running cost is the gel (anti-freeze) membrane used on the skin for each handle placement. Ask the supplier for the price of membranes, how many placements one pack covers, and the cost of spare handles. These decide your cost per session more than the machine price does.

Is Cryolipolysis Safe? Side Effects to Know

Common short-term effects are redness, bruising, swelling, numbness and soreness, which usually settle within a few weeks. A rare effect, paradoxical adipose hyperplasia (the treated fat grows instead of shrinking), has been estimated at about 1 in 20,000 treatments. Treat only suitable patients, follow the maker's protocol and train staff well.

What to Check Before Buying a Cryolipolysis Machine

  • Number of handles and the sizes included in the price
  • Lowest temperature, temperature sensors and auto shut-off
  • Price and supply of gel membranes in India
  • Warranty on the compressor and handles
  • On-site installation, staff training and protocols
  • Service response time in your city
