Pulse Ultra-Lightweight Foldable Wheelchair: Joylite-1 & Joylite-2

When transport and storage are the deciding factor — not terrain, not maximum range — an ultra-lightweight foldable chair is the right category. Joylite is built specifically for that priority.

What is the Pulse Joylite wheelchair? The Pulse Joylite-1 and Joylite-2 are ultra-lightweight, foldable electric wheelchairs designed for easy transport and daily mobility. Joylite-2 offers rear-wheel or front-wheel drive options with 250W dual DC motors, a 12Ah battery, and a 100 kg load capacity.

Who It's For

Joylite suits users who:

  • Fold and unfold their wheelchair frequently through the day
  • Need a chair that's genuinely easy for one person to lift and store
  • Use the wheelchair primarily indoors or on well-maintained outdoor surfaces
  • Are focused on daily mobility support with an active, engaged rehabilitation routine

How to Use It

Joylite-1 features a foldable frame with extended battery life, built for active rehabilitation use. Joylite-2 offers a choice between rear-wheel and front-wheel drive configurations depending on the manoeuvrability the user needs, powered by 250W dual DC motors with a 12Ah battery for a range of at least 10 km. Obstacle clearance of 40mm or more means it handles typical thresholds and small kerbs without difficulty, at a maximum speed of 6 km/h.

Why Choose Joylite Over Other Models

If your main frustration with a wheelchair is the daily hassle of folding, lifting, and storing it, Joylite's ultra-lightweight design addresses that directly. It trades some of the range and load capacity of heavier models for genuine day-to-day convenience — the right trade-off if your routine involves frequent folding rather than long-distance outdoor travel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who should choose an ultra-lightweight wheelchair like Joylite?

Users who prioritise easy daily transport and storage above all else, including those who travel frequently or have limited storage space at home.

What drive options does the Joylite-2 offer?

Joylite-2 (models WT-100W/WT-100WA/WT-100WB) offers rear-wheel or front-wheel drive options, with 250W dual DC motors, a 12Ah battery, and load capacity of 100 kg.

What is the obstacle clearance on Joylite-2?

Joylite-2 has an obstacle clearance of 40mm or more, with a maximum speed of 6 km/h and a range of at least 10 km per charge.
