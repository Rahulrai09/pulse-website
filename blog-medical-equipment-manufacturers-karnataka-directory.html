Medical Equipment Manufacturers in Bangalore and Karnataka: Complete 2026 Directory

"Medical equipment company Bangalore" turns up a mix of manufacturers, resellers, service agencies, and directory listings with no clear labelling of which is which. This is a category-based directory that separates them — so you know exactly what kind of company you're looking at before you reach out.

Bangalore Versus Bengaluru: Both Get Searched

The city's official name is Bengaluru, but "Bangalore" remains the more commonly searched term, especially outside Karnataka. This directory uses Bengaluru in the body text and includes Bangalore in headings and search-facing copy, since both are genuinely how people look for this information.

How to Use This Directory

Before contacting any company from a list like this — ours included — it's worth understanding what type of entity you're dealing with. This directory separates:

  • Manufacturers — companies that design, build, and hold regulatory responsibility for the equipment
  • OEM and contract manufacturers — companies that build to another brand's specification
  • Medical-device component suppliers — supplying parts into other manufacturers' products, not finished equipment to hospitals
  • Distributors and dealers — reselling manufacturers' products, not building them
  • Hospital-equipment service companies — installation, maintenance, and repair, often independent of the manufacturer
  • Incubators, clusters and public-sector support institutions — not vendors themselves, but useful for discovering who operates in the region

Medical Equipment Manufacturing Areas in Bengaluru

Medical-device manufacturing and related activity in Bengaluru is concentrated in a handful of industrial and technology zones:

  • Peenya Industrial Area
  • Bommasandra Industrial Area
  • Electronic City
  • Whitefield and EPIP Zone
  • Bannerghatta Road and Hulimavu
  • Hosur Road
  • Rajajinagar
  • Madiwala and central Bengaluru

Beyond city limits, Mysuru and other Karnataka locations also host medical-device manufacturing activity, though at smaller scale than Bengaluru's clusters.

Manufacturer, Supplier, Dealer, or Distributor?

These terms get used loosely in search results and even in some companies' own marketing. A quick way to tell the difference: ask who holds the CDSCO manufacturing licence for the specific product you're buying. If the company you're talking to isn't the licence holder, you're talking to a reseller or service partner — which isn't necessarily a problem, but you should know which relationship you're entering.

How to Verify a Company Before You Buy

Whether sourcing from this directory or any other, confirm the following before finalising a purchase:

  • Company name, city, and industrial area
  • Manufacturer, OEM, distributor, or service-provider status
  • Product category and example products
  • CDSCO licence and applicable device classification
  • ISO 13485 certification and its scope
  • Whether the company supplies hospitals directly, or only dealers/labs/export markets
  • Installation and service coverage in your area
  • Last-verified date on any listing you're relying on, including this one

Be cautious of directory listings — including third-party aggregator sites — that don't clearly distinguish manufacturers from resellers, or that don't show when the information was last checked.

A directory is a starting point for discovery, not a substitute for verifying the specific company and product you're about to commit to.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a complete list of every medical equipment manufacturer in Karnataka?

No. This directory highlights companies with documented relevance to specific categories; it isn't an exhaustive registry. For a comprehensive list, official CDSCO records and state industry-cluster directories are more complete, though they require independent verification too.

How is this different from Pulse's existing guide to suppliers in Bengaluru?

That guide focuses on how to choose and evaluate a supplier — compliance, tenders, service terms. This directory is organised around discovering which manufacturers and related entities operate in the region and what category they work in.

Should I trust a company's self-reported manufacturer status?

Not without verification. Ask for the specific CDSCO licence tied to the product you're buying, and confirm the licence holder matches the company you're purchasing from.
