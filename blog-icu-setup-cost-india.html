ICU Setup Cost in India: Equipment Budget for a 10-Bed ICU

What a 10-Bed ICU Needs

India’s critical care doctors (the ISCCM) published a consensus on ICU planning in 2020. For a Level II ICU, it suggests:

  • Ventilators for about half the beds, and non-invasive (BiPAP) units for about a quarter.
  • One multipara monitor per bed, ideally linked to a central station.
  • Syringe pumps: 2 to 3 per bed. Infusion pumps: about 1 per bed.
  • Gas outlets per bed: 2 oxygen, 1 to 2 vacuum and 1 to 2 compressed air.
  • Power: many sockets per bed, with at least half on UPS backup.
  • Shared items: a crash cart and defibrillator, ECG machine, blood gas analyser and portable suction.

Space and Staff Planning

  • Space: at least 150 sq ft per open bed, or 200 to 250 sq ft for a single room, plus 100 to 150% more for support areas.
  • Size: 8 to 12 beds per unit works best.
  • How many ICU beds: at least 5% of the hospital’s beds, up to 25% in large tertiary hospitals.
  • Nurses: one nurse per ventilated patient is ideal; one nurse for two or three less sick patients is acceptable.
  • Doctors: one doctor should not look after more than five critically ill patients.

Costs People Forget

  • Building and air conditioning. Civil work, flooring, isolation rooms and HVAC are outside the equipment budget. For scale, a 50-bed government critical care block in Rajamahendravaram was estimated at Rs 23.75 crore under PM-ABHIM.
  • Oxygen source. The pipeline budget does not include an oxygen plant or manifold room.
  • Consumables. Ventilator circuits, sensors, syringes and infusion sets are ongoing costs.
  • Service. Plan for warranty and annual maintenance contracts on ventilators and monitors.
  • Running cost. A 2021 AIIMS Bhubaneswar study found a surgical ICU bed cost about Rs 11,241 per day to run, with staff the largest share.

How to Reduce ICU Setup Cost

  • Buy one ICU ventilator for every two beds, plus BiPAP units, instead of a ventilator for every bed.
  • Use advanced monitors (with EtCO2) only where ventilated patients are, and mid-range monitors elsewhere.
  • Choose 5-function electric beds for the ICU and 3-function beds for the step-down unit.
  • Buy from one supplier for ventilators, monitors, pumps, beds and pipeline, so one team answers for installation and service.
  • Check service response times and spare-part stock in your city before comparing prices.

ICU Setup Cost in India: FAQs

How much does it cost to set up an ICU in India?

The main equipment and medical gas pipeline for a 10-bed ICU cost about Rs 70 lakh to Rs 2 crore, or roughly Rs 7 lakh to Rs 20 lakh per bed, based on 2026 Indian price guides. Building work, air conditioning, staff and consumables are extra.

How many ventilators does a 10-bed ICU need?

The ISCCM 2020 consensus suggests ventilators for about half the ICU beds (and at least two per unit), plus non-invasive ventilators for about a quarter of beds. For 10 beds, that is about 5 ICU ventilators and 2 to 3 BiPAP units.

How much space does one ICU bed need?

The ISCCM 2020 consensus recommends at least 150 sq ft of patient area per open ICU bed and 200 to 250 sq ft for a single room, plus 100 to 150% extra for support areas such as the nursing station and stores.

How many ICU beds should a hospital have?

ISCCM recommends that at least 5% of a hospital’s beds are ICU beds, and up to 25% in tertiary hospitals. Units of 8 to 12 beds work best; larger hospitals add separate ICUs rather than one big unit.

What does it cost to run an ICU bed each day?

A 2021 costing study at AIIMS Bhubaneswar found a surgical ICU bed cost about Rs 11,241 per day to run, with staff about 43% of the cost and equipment about Rs 4,088 per day.
