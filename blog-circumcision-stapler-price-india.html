What Is Stapler Circumcision?

In stapler circumcision, the surgeon uses a single-use device that cuts the foreskin and closes the edge with a ring of small staples in one step. It replaces cutting with scissors and stitching by hand. The device is used once and thrown away, so there are no reusable instruments to sterilise.

What Does the Evidence Say?

A 2024 study in Frontiers in Pediatrics compared a disposable circumcision device with traditional surgery in 301 children. With the device, operating time was about 10 minutes versus about 21 minutes, and blood loss about 4 mL versus about 10 mL. Wound healing took longer with the device, about 14 days versus about 8 days. Read our full evidence review.

What Changes the Cost of Stapler Circumcision?

  • Hospital type. Small clinics cost less than multi-speciality and super-speciality hospitals.
  • Anaesthesia. Local anaesthesia costs less than general anaesthesia, which is common in young children.
  • City. Metro hospitals usually charge more.
  • Tests, room and medicines. Consultation, blood tests, day-care or overnight stay and dressings add to the bill.

What Should a Clinic Check Before Buying Circumcision Staplers?

  • Sizes available for children and adults, and a sizing guide
  • Sterile, single-use packing with lot number and expiry date
  • Staple and ring material
  • Regulatory papers for the device
  • Surgeon training and a written protocol
  • Steady supply and price for bulk orders

Circumcision Stapler Price in India: FAQs

How much does a circumcision stapler cost in India?

Disposable circumcision staplers list at about Rs 3,000 to Rs 3,300 per device in India (IndiaMART listings, October 2026). Bulk orders from hospitals are usually quoted separately.

How much does stapler circumcision cost in India?

Circumcision in a private hospital costs about Rs 25,000 to Rs 45,000 on average. Stapler circumcision in Hyderabad is quoted at about Rs 30,000 to Rs 60,000, from small clinics to super-speciality hospitals (HexaHealth, 2024).

Is stapler circumcision faster than traditional circumcision?

In a 2024 Frontiers in Pediatrics study of 301 children, operating time was about 10 minutes with a disposable device versus about 21 minutes with traditional surgery, and blood loss was about 4 mL versus 10 mL.

Does stapler circumcision heal faster?

Not in the 2024 study: wound healing took about 14 days with the device versus about 8 days with traditional surgery. Ask your surgeon what to expect.

Is a circumcision stapler reusable?

No. It is a single-use, sterile device. A new one is used for every patient, so there is nothing to clean or sterilise afterwards.
