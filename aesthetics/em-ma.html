Pulse Em-Ma

Em-Ma uses High-Intensity Focused Electromagnetic (HIFEM) technology to deliver thousands of supramaximal pelvic floor muscle contractions in a single session, restoring neuromuscular control without surgery or downtime. A typical 30-minute session induces around 12,000 contractions, and clinical follow-up across 100+ cases over two years found 95% of patients reported a meaningful improvement in quality of life. It treats both women (post-natal weakness, post-hysterectomy, post-menopause) and men (post-prostate and bladder-related weakness), and patients remain fully clothed throughout.

Who Em-Ma Is For

Urinary incontinence affects an estimated 60% of women between the ages of 45 and 55, and around 15% of men over 60 — yet it remains under-treated because most non-surgical options are limited or short-lived. Em-Ma addresses both groups directly: in women, it supports recovery after pregnancy, childbirth, hysterectomy, and menopause; in men, it helps with post-prostate treatment weakness, bladder-related issues, and sphincter control. Because the HIFEM contractions are induced electromagnetically rather than through active exercise, sessions are comfortable, require no undressing, and fit easily into a clinic's existing treatment schedule.
