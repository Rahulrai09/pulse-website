HYDRA FACIAL | SKIN HYDRATION | DEEP CLEANSING

Pulse HYDRAFRAC

Hydrafrac combines several skincare steps most clinics would otherwise need separate machines for — exfoliation, extraction, and hydration — through one handpiece. Its signature VADRIFY technology (Variable Abrasion, Dynamic Rolling, and Infusion) drives a vacuum-assisted microneedling tip that clears clogged pores while infusing serums deep into the skin to boost collagen and reduce fine lines. The micro-channels it creates dramatically improve how well active ingredients absorb, making it a versatile platform across face, body, and scalp treatments.

One Machine, Multiple Treatments

Because Hydrafrac handles cleansing, extraction, and infusion in one pass, clinics can offer a complete facial without switching between separate devices for each step. Treatments are typically finished with a customized serum set tailored to the skin's needs — hydrating, brightening, or calming — so the same platform adapts to a wide range of client concerns. It works equally well as a standalone signature treatment or as prep before other in-clinic procedures.
