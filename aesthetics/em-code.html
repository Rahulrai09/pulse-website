How Em Code Fits Your Clinic

HIFEM body contouring makes muscles contract strongly while RF heats the fat layer. It suits patients who want firmer abdomen, buttocks, arms or thighs, and pairs well with cryolipolysis (Cool Shape) for clinics building a body contouring service.

What to Check Before Buying

  • Applicators. Check how many areas can be treated at once and the applicator sizes.
  • Protocols. Ask for the session plan and how many sessions a typical course needs.
  • Training. Ask what operator training is included and whether it covers settings for Indian skin types.
  • Certifications and documents. Ask for the certificates and import documents for the exact model you buy.
  • Running costs. List every consumable, tip, lamp or handpiece that wears out, with prices.
  • Warranty and service. Confirm what the warranty covers, especially handpieces, and how fast an engineer reaches your clinic.
  • Space and power. Check room size, power supply and any cooling or ventilation needs.
