Pulse Skin Analyzer

The Skin Analyzer combines a 36-megapixel industrial-grade camera with spectral imaging to map sebum levels, hydration, fine lines, texture, sun damage, and pigmentation across both the surface and deeper layers of the skin. An external moisture-reading pen adds precision to hydration assessment, while built-in blue-light sterilization keeps the device hygienic between patients. The result is an objective, before-and-after record that helps practitioners recommend the right treatment and demonstrate real progress over time.

Why Clinics Use the Skin Analyzer

Recommending the right treatment starts with knowing exactly what the skin needs — not guessing from a visual check. The Skin Analyzer turns that first consultation into a data-backed process, giving practitioners a clear baseline reading and a way to show clients measurable progress after each course of treatment. It pairs naturally with facial and body treatment machines already in a clinic, helping justify treatment plans and track results in a way clients can actually see for themselves.
