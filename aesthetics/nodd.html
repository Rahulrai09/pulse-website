RF MICRONEEDLE | COLLAGEN REMODELING

Pulse Nodd

Nodd pairs microneedling with radiofrequency energy (MNRF) to tighten and resurface skin with far less downtime than fully ablative treatments. Gold-plated needles deliver RF energy at an adjustable depth to target fine lines and acne scars, and a dual air-chamber design in the handpiece helps topical serums penetrate up to 67% deeper into the dermis right after treatment. Two applicator sizes cover small and large treatment areas, and the precision of the microneedle channels carries a much lower risk of burns or hyperpigmentation than optical treatments like IPL or laser.

A Safer Option Across Skin Tones

Because Nodd delivers energy directly through microneedle channels rather than broad optical light, it carries less risk of triggering hyperpigmentation in darker skin tones — a common limitation with laser or IPL-based resurfacing. That makes it a versatile choice for clinics serving a diverse client base who want visible improvement in scarring, texture, and fine lines without excluding any skin type from treatment.
