Pulse Ultra-Lightweight Foldable Wheelchair: Pulse UltraLight & Pulse MiniLite

When transport and storage are the deciding factor — not terrain, not maximum range — an ultra-lightweight foldable chair is the right category. The Pulse UltraLight and MiniLite are built specifically for that priority.

What are the Pulse UltraLight and MiniLite? The Pulse UltraLight and Pulse MiniLite are Pulse's lightest folding electric wheelchairs, at just 19 kg without the battery. Both have an aluminium frame, carry up to 120 kg, run about 15 km per charge on a 24V 10Ah battery, and use puncture-free tyres.

Who Needs the Pulse UltraLight and MiniLite

The Pulse UltraLight and MiniLite suit users who:

  • Want the lightest possible electric wheelchair to lift into a car
  • Weigh up to 120 kg
  • Do not want to check tyre pressure or fix punctures
  • Use the chair indoors, on footpaths and while travelling

Pulse UltraLight or Pulse MiniLite?

Both weigh 19 kg without the battery, with two 250 W motors, a top speed of 6 km/h and electromagnetic brakes. Pulse UltraLight has a five-speed joystick, a removable battery and small 7-inch front and 11-inch rear wheels, so it folds to about 44 x 58 x 72 cm. Pulse MiniLite has a breathable mesh seat and larger 10-inch front and 16-inch rear wheels for a smoother ride outdoors.

Pulse Ultra-Lightweight Foldable Wheelchair: Key Facts

  • Pulse UltraLight and Pulse MiniLite are Pulse's lightest foldable electric wheelchairs.
  • Both use a 24V 10Ah lithium-ion battery, about 240 Wh.
  • 240 Wh is under the 300 Wh airline limit for a removed wheelchair battery.
  • WHO's 2023 Wheelchair Provision Guidelines recommend a personal assessment, proper fitting, user training and follow-up for every wheelchair user.

Flying with the Pulse UltraLight and MiniLite

Airlines care about how much energy a wheelchair battery stores, measured in watt-hours (Wh). You work it out as volts × amp-hours. The Pulse UltraLight and MiniLite use a 24V 10Ah lithium-ion battery, which is about 240 Wh. That is under the 300 Wh limit that airline safety rules set for a battery removed from a wheelchair, which makes air travel simpler.

Always tell your airline before you fly. Removed and spare batteries must go in the cabin with their terminals covered. You may carry one spare battery up to 300 Wh, or two spares up to 160 Wh each.

Pulse Ultra-Lightweight Foldable Wheelchair: FAQs

How much do the Pulse UltraLight and MiniLite weigh?

Both Pulse UltraLight and Pulse MiniLite weigh 19 kg without the battery, making them Pulse's lightest electric wheelchairs.

What's the difference between Pulse UltraLight and Pulse MiniLite?

Pulse UltraLight has a five-speed joystick, a removable battery and smaller 7-inch and 11-inch wheels. Pulse MiniLite has a breathable mesh seat and larger 10-inch and 16-inch wheels. Both carry up to 120 kg and run about 15 km per charge.

Do the Pulse UltraLight and MiniLite get punctures?

No. Both models use puncture-free, non-pneumatic tyres, so there is no air to check and no punctures to fix.
