Medical Gas Pipeline Cost in India: Per Bed, Per OT and Per Hospital

A medical gas pipeline system (MGPS) brings oxygen, medical air and suction to every bed. This guide gives indicative costs per bed, per theatre and per hospital, what is included, and what to plan before installation.

How much does a medical gas pipeline cost in India? Indicative ranges published by RayMedico Projects put the pipeline at about Rs 25,000 to Rs 45,000 per general ward bed, Rs 1.2 lakh to Rs 2.2 lakh per ICU bed and Rs 3.5 lakh to Rs 7 lakh per operation theatre. For whole-hospital distribution they give about Rs 18 lakh to Rs 35 lakh for 50 beds and Rs 35 lakh to Rs 70 lakh for 100 beds, excluding the oxygen plant.

What a Medical Gas Pipeline Includes

A medical gas pipeline system has three parts. The source supplies the gas: cylinder manifolds with automatic changeover, an oxygen plant or a liquid oxygen tank, plus medical air and vacuum plants. The distribution is copper pipework with zone valves and alarms. The outlets are where staff connect equipment, on bed head panels, vertical columns or ceiling pendants.

What Drives the Cost

1. Number and type of beds

ICU and OT beds need more gases and more outlets than ward beds, so they cost several times more per bed.

2. Gas source

Cylinder manifolds cost least to install. An oxygen plant or liquid oxygen tank costs more up front but can lower running costs for bigger hospitals.

3. Bed head panels or pendants

Wall panels suit wards. Ceiling pendants keep ICU and OT floors clear but cost more.

4. Alarms, valves and testing

Zone valves, pressure alarms and proper testing add cost but are essential for safety and accreditation.

5. Maintenance

RayMedico puts annual maintenance at about 6 to 10 percent of the system's capital value.

Medical Gas Pipeline Cost in India: FAQs

How much does a medical gas pipeline cost per bed in India?

Indicative ranges published by RayMedico Projects put it at about Rs 25,000 to Rs 45,000 per general ward bed, Rs 60,000 to Rs 1.1 lakh per HDU bed and Rs 1.2 lakh to Rs 2.2 lakh per ICU bed.

How much does a medical gas pipeline cost for a 100-bed hospital?

About Rs 35 lakh to Rs 70 lakh for distribution across a 100-bed hospital, excluding the oxygen plant, liquid oxygen tank and pendants, according to the same indicative ranges.

What does a medical gas pipeline system include?

A gas source (manifolds, oxygen plant or liquid oxygen tank, air and vacuum plants), copper pipework with zone valves and alarms, and outlets on bed head panels, columns or pendants.

Is a cylinder manifold or an oxygen plant better?

Cylinder manifolds cost least to install and suit smaller hospitals. An oxygen plant or liquid oxygen tank costs more up front but can lower running costs for larger hospitals.

How much does maintenance cost?

RayMedico puts annual maintenance at about 6 to 10 percent of the system's capital value.
