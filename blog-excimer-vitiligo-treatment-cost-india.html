Excimer Laser for Vitiligo: Cost per Session and Sessions Needed

Excimer is a targeted light treatment for white patches of vitiligo. This guide shows what a session costs in India, how many sessions people usually need, which body areas respond best and how it compares with whole-body phototherapy.

Excimer treatment for vitiligo costs about Rs 2,000 to Rs 8,000 per session in India, according to an insurer's guide updated in May 2026. Sessions are usually given twice a week. In one study, patches took about 17 sessions on average to start regaining colour, and the face and neck responded best. A full course can cost Rs 40,000 to Rs 1.6 lakh (our estimate).

These ranges come from an insurer's vitiligo guide updated in May 2026. A Mumbai skin clinic lists excimer at about Rs 2,000 to Rs 5,000 or more per session. The price depends on the size and number of patches and the city.

How Many Sessions Are Needed?

In a 2014 study of 105 vitiligo patches treated twice a week:

  • Patches took about 17 sessions on average to start regaining colour.
  • About 20 sessions on average were needed for more than 75% colour return.
  • 60% of patches reached more than 75% colour return within up to 48 sessions.

Results vary a lot from person to person. Your dermatologist will decide how long to continue.

These figures are from the same 2014 study. The face and neck usually respond best. Hands and feet respond poorly, so people with patches there should discuss other options with their doctor.

Excimer vs Whole-Body Phototherapy

Excimer (308 nm)Narrowband UVB phototherapy
Area treatedOnly the white patchLarge areas or the whole body
Best forSmall, stable patchesWidespread vitiligo
Cost per sessionRs 2,000 to Rs 8,000Rs 1,500 to Rs 5,000

Because excimer treats only the patch, the healthy skin around it gets no extra UV light. Excimer lamps and excimer lasers both use 308 nm light, and a 2018 study notes they work about equally well for vitiligo.

Side Effects

In the 2014 study, side effects were mild: redness in 61% of patches, itching in 32%, and small blisters or peeling in about 9%. All settled within 3 to 5 days after treatment ended. Protect treated skin from the sun as your doctor advises.

Does Insurance Cover It?

The insurer's guide says health insurance may cover vitiligo treatment when a dermatologist finds it medically necessary, subject to policy terms and waiting periods. If vitiligo was diagnosed before you bought the policy, a pre-existing disease waiting period of up to 3 years may apply. Check your own policy before starting.

What is the cost of excimer laser treatment for vitiligo in India?
