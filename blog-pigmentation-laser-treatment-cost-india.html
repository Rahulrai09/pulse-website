Pigmentation Laser Treatment Cost in India: Laser, IPL and Peels

Dark spots, tanning, freckles and melasma are among the most common skin concerns in India. This guide compares what laser, IPL and peel treatments cost per session, how many sessions they need, and the downtime.

Q-switched laser sessions cost about Rs 5,000 to Rs 18,000 depending on the clinic, IPL about Rs 4,000 to Rs 15,000, and chemical peels about Rs 3,000 to Rs 8,000. Most need 3 to 6 sessions.

Total Cost of a Course

Because most treatments need several sessions, compare the full course. As a rough guide (our estimate), 4 to 6 Q-switched sessions at Rs 8,000 cost Rs 32,000 to Rs 48,000, and 6 to 8 peels at Rs 5,000 cost Rs 30,000 to Rs 40,000. Packages usually lower the price per session.

Which Treatment for Which Concern?

  • Tanning and dull skin: Q-switched laser toning, IPL or light peels.
  • Freckles and sun spots: Q-switched or pico laser, or IPL.
  • Melasma: needs a dermatologist's plan; often creams first, then gentle laser or peels. Melasma can come back, so sun protection is essential.
  • Tattoo removal: Q-switched or pico laser; many sessions, depending on ink colour and depth.

Pico vs Q-Switched Laser

Both break up pigment with very short laser pulses. Pico lasers use even shorter pulses (picoseconds), which can break pigment into smaller pieces, often in fewer sessions, with minimal downtime. Q-switched lasers are older, widely available and usually cheaper per session. See our picosecond laser machine price guide for how the machines compare.

Safety on Indian Skin

  • Darker skin can react to strong heat or light with more pigmentation, so settings must be gentle and adjusted to your skin type.
  • Use sunscreen every day during and after treatment.
  • Choose a qualified dermatologist and ask which machine will be used.

Insurance usually does not cover pigmentation treatment, as it is treated as cosmetic.

Pigmentation Treatment Cost: FAQs

What is the cost of laser treatment for pigmentation in India?

About Rs 5,000 to Rs 18,000 a session, according to a 2026 hospital guide. The price depends on the laser, the area and the clinic.

How many sessions are needed for pigmentation?

Usually 3 to 6 laser or IPL sessions, or 6 to 8 chemical peels, depending on the type and depth of pigmentation.

Is pico laser better than Q-switched laser for pigmentation?

Pico lasers use shorter pulses and often need fewer sessions with minimal downtime. Q-switched lasers are widely available and usually cheaper per session. A dermatologist should choose.

Is laser pigmentation treatment safe for Indian skin?

Yes, with gentle settings chosen for your skin type, a qualified dermatologist and daily sunscreen. Strong settings can cause more pigmentation on darker skin.

Can melasma be removed permanently?

Melasma can be lightened, but it often comes back, especially with sun exposure. It needs a dermatologist's long-term plan and daily sun protection.
