Autoclave Price in India: Portable, Vertical, Horizontal and Class B

Autoclaves in India range from a few thousand rupees to over Rs 10 lakh. This guide explains the price bands by type, how to choose capacity, and what clinics and hospitals should check before buying.

How much does an autoclave cost in India? Bajaj Finserv's September 2026 guide puts portable (cooker type) autoclaves at about Rs 5,500 to Rs 55,000, vertical autoclaves at Rs 7,000 to Rs 2.55 lakh, tabletop autoclaves at Rs 9,500 to Rs 5.5 lakh or more, horizontal autoclaves at Rs 20,000 to over Rs 10 lakh, and Class B autoclaves at Rs 70,000 to over Rs 5 lakh.

Which Type Do You Need?

  • Portable and vertical: simple, affordable and good for unwrapped instruments and small loads.
  • Horizontal: front-loading with larger chambers and trays, used for CSSD and OT loads.
  • Class B: uses a vacuum pump to remove air before sterilising, so steam reaches inside wrapped packs, hollow instruments and porous loads. Increasingly expected in dental and surgical practice.

What Drives the Price

1. Chamber capacity

Larger chambers, from a few litres to well over 100 litres, cost more but process more instruments per cycle.

2. Cycle class and vacuum

Gravity (N type) autoclaves cost less. Vacuum (Class B) models cost more and handle a wider range of loads.

3. Material

Stainless steel chambers last longer and resist corrosion better than aluminium.

4. Controls, safety and records

Microprocessor control, door safety locks, pressure and temperature displays, printers and data logs help with infection control audits.

5. Electric or non-electric

Non-electric models heat on a gas stove and suit areas with unreliable power. Electric models are easier to control.

Autoclave Price in India: FAQs

How much does an autoclave cost in India?

Bajaj Finserv's September 2026 guide puts portable autoclaves at about Rs 5,500 to Rs 55,000, vertical autoclaves at Rs 7,000 to Rs 2.55 lakh, tabletop autoclaves at Rs 9,500 to Rs 5.5 lakh or more, horizontal autoclaves at Rs 20,000 to over Rs 10 lakh, and Class B autoclaves at Rs 70,000 to over Rs 5 lakh.

What is the difference between a vertical and horizontal autoclave?

A vertical autoclave loads from the top and suits smaller clinics and labs. A horizontal autoclave loads from the front, holds larger trays and is used in hospital CSSDs and OT complexes.

What is a Class B autoclave?

A Class B autoclave uses a vacuum pump to remove air before and after sterilising, so steam can reach inside wrapped packs, hollow instruments such as handpieces, and porous loads. It is the highest class of small steam sterilisers.

What size autoclave does a clinic need?

Base it on how many instrument sets you process per day and the largest tray you use. Small clinics often manage with a 12 to 25 litre unit; hospitals need larger horizontal autoclaves for CSSD.

How do I know the autoclave has sterilised properly?

Use chemical indicators on every load and biological indicators regularly, check the pressure, temperature and time of each cycle, and keep records. Follow your infection control policy.
