What Makes One Chair Cost More Than Another

1. How heavy the chair is

A lighter chair is easier to lift into a car. Light metals like aluminium cost more than steel. That is why the lightest chairs are often not the cheapest ones.

2. The battery

Lithium batteries are light and last for years. Lead-acid batteries are cheaper but heavy, and they wear out sooner. All Pulse electric wheelchairs use lithium batteries.

3. How it folds

Some chairs fold by hand. Some fold with one click. The most costly ones fold by themselves when you press a button on a remote.

4. Comfort features

A back that leans back, a headrest, springs for bumpy roads and a wider seat all add to the price. They matter a lot if the person sits in the chair for many hours a day.

5. How much weight it can carry

Chairs made for heavier users need a stronger frame and a bigger battery. That raises the price.

6. Service and spare parts

A cheap chair is not cheap if nobody can fix it. Ask who will repair it, how fast, and whether spare parts are kept in India. Read more in our guide on why after-sales service matters.

Extra Costs to Plan For

  • GST: wheelchairs, including electric ones, have 5% GST in India.
  • New battery: a lithium battery usually lasts about 3 to 5 years. A new one costs about Rs 15,000 to Rs 35,000.
  • Tyres: air-filled tyres can get a puncture. Puncture-free tyres save this cost.
  • Repairs after warranty: ask what the warranty covers and for how long.

Before you buy: a quick checklist

  1. Weigh the person. Pick a chair that carries more than their weight.
  2. Measure the car boot and the doors at home. Check the folded size fits.
  3. Ask who will lift the chair. If it is an older family member, pick a light one.
  4. Ask for a test ride on the roads you use every day.
  5. Ask if the price includes GST, delivery and a charger.
  6. Ask about the warranty, battery cost and who repairs it near you.

Electric Wheelchair Price in India: FAQs

How much does an electric wheelchair cost in India?

Most electric wheelchairs in India cost between Rs 35,000 and Rs 2.5 lakh. Basic chairs start at about Rs 35,000, light foldable chairs cost about Rs 50,000 to Rs 1.5 lakh, and heavy-duty chairs can cost up to Rs 2.5 lakh.

Is there GST on electric wheelchairs?

Yes. Wheelchairs, including electric wheelchairs, have 5% GST in India. Some sellers show prices without GST, so ask before you compare.

How long does the battery last, and what does a new one cost?

A lithium battery usually lasts about 3 to 5 years. A new one costs about Rs 15,000 to Rs 35,000. Lead-acid batteries are cheaper but wear out faster.

Which electric wheelchair is best for car travel?

Pick a light chair that folds small. The Pulse UltraLight and MiniLite weigh 19 kg without the battery, and the Pulse Everyday and SmartFold weigh 22 kg and fold with one click or a remote.
