Why Infusion Accuracy Is Non-Negotiable

An infusion pump has one job: deliver the exact dose, at the exact rate, for as long as the order says. Get that wrong by even a small margin on a vasopressor or a pediatric drip, and the consequences are immediate. That is why flow accuracy and occlusion detection are the two numbers we check first on any pump before it goes near a patient, not the brochure's feature list.

This pump also protects against free flow, the uncontrolled gravity drip that can happen if the line is removed from the pump incorrectly. That single safeguard has prevented more than one serious incident in hospitals we work with. Add a battery that holds charge through a patient transfer between wards or into an ambulance, and you get a device that keeps dosing steady exactly when steady dosing matters most: during the handoff, not just at the bedside.
