Five Parameters, One Screen, No Guesswork

A general ward or step-down unit does not need every bell and whistle a cath lab monitor has. What it needs is ECG, SpO2, NIBP, temperature, and respiration rate, read accurately and displayed clearly, without a nurse having to dig through menus mid-shift. That is the entire design brief behind this monitor, and it shows in how quickly new staff pick it up.

We also built it to hold up through a busy night shift: alarms that are loud enough to hear over ward noise but not so sensitive that they go off every time a patient shifts position, and a screen that stays readable from across the room. For hospitals running a central nurse-call setup, it integrates cleanly, so a reading on this monitor does not sit isolated from the rest of the ward's monitoring picture.
