When Two More Parameters Change the Clinical Picture

A 7-parameter monitor adds invasive blood pressure and EtCO2 on top of the standard ECG, SpO2, NIBP, temperature, and respiration set. In a cath lab, OT, or high-dependency ICU, those two extra readings are often the ones that change a clinical decision in real time, which is why most hospitals running these settings choose this over a basic 5-parameter unit.

It is built to sit inside a hospital's existing monitoring network rather than as a standalone box: central monitoring station compatibility and printer output are both included, so a reading taken at the bedside is also visible to the team at the nurse's station. For departments upgrading from 5-parameter monitors, this is usually the natural next step rather than a full workflow change.
