FAQ

Who We Serve FAQs

Still have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Who does Pulse sell medical equipment to?

Pulse sells to new and existing hospitals, nursing homes, clinics, dialysis centres, labs, aesthetic clinics, government buyers and home users across India. Dealers, distributors and hospital suppliers can also partner with Pulse.

Can Pulse set up a new hospital from scratch?

Yes. Pulse sets up hospitals from 0 to 100: planning each department, equipment, modular OTs, medical gas pipelines, installation, staff training and after-sales service.

I already bought from Pulse. How do I get service?

Message +91 90711 01108 on WhatsApp or email info@pulseio.in with the product and serial number. Pulse offers installation, staff training, AMC and CMC plans, repairs and spare parts, with a 96-hour service response across India.

Can dealers and distributors sell Pulse products?

Yes. Dealers, distributors and hospital suppliers can apply through the Pulse contact page by choosing "Dealer or partnership", or by messaging +91 90711 01108 on WhatsApp.

Does Pulse supply government hospitals?

Yes. Pulse products meet GeM (Government e-Marketplace) portal requirements and national procurement standards. Ask the team for the certificates your tender needs.

Can I buy a Pulse wheelchair for home use?

Yes. Pulse electric wheelchairs are sold online at pulsemotion.in with home delivery. There are 11 models, from light folding chairs to heavy-duty and reclining chairs.
