Pulse Autofold Electric Wheelchair: Pulse Everyday & Pulse SmartFold

For people who travel often, a wheelchair's weight and how fast it folds can decide whether a trip feels easy or tiring. The Pulse Everyday and SmartFold are built for exactly that: a light aluminium frame and quick folding for car boots and flights.

What are the Pulse Everyday and SmartFold? The Pulse Everyday and Pulse SmartFold are folding electric wheelchairs built for travel. Both have a 22 kg aluminium frame (without battery), carry up to 120 kg and run about 15 km per charge on a 24V 10Ah battery. Pulse Everyday folds with one click; Pulse SmartFold folds by itself with a remote. Both are listed as IATA compliant.

Who Needs the Pulse Everyday and SmartFold

The Pulse Everyday and SmartFold suit users who:

  • Travel often by car or air and need quick, simple folding
  • Want a light chair that a family member can lift into a car boot
  • Weigh up to 120 kg
  • Mostly use the chair on footpaths, malls, airports and indoors

How the Pulse Everyday and SmartFold Fit Your Routine

Both models have a 22 kg aluminium frame (without the battery) in a carbon fiber colour finish, a comfortable 43 x 43 cm mesh seat, joystick control and electromagnetic brakes that hold the chair still when you stop. Two 250 W motors give a top speed of 6 km/h and about 15 km per charge.

The difference is the fold. Pulse Everyday folds with one click and rolls along like luggage. Pulse SmartFold folds and opens by itself at the press of a button on its remote, which helps users who cannot bend or lift easily.

Pulse Autofold Electric Wheelchair: Key Facts

  • Pulse Everyday folds for car boots and flights; Pulse SmartFold folds with one touch.
  • Both use a 24V 10Ah lithium-ion battery, about 240 Wh.
  • 240 Wh is under the 300 Wh airline limit for a removed wheelchair battery.
  • WHO's 2023 Wheelchair Provision Guidelines recommend a personal assessment, proper fitting, user training and follow-up for every wheelchair user.

Flying with the Pulse Everyday and SmartFold

Airlines care about how much energy a wheelchair battery stores, measured in watt-hours (Wh). You work it out as volts × amp-hours. The Pulse Everyday and SmartFold use a 24V 10Ah lithium-ion battery, which is about 240 Wh. That is under the 300 Wh limit that airline safety rules set for a battery removed from a wheelchair, which makes air travel simpler.

Always tell your airline before you fly. Removed and spare batteries must go in the cabin with their terminals covered. You may carry one spare battery up to 300 Wh, or two spares up to 160 Wh each.
