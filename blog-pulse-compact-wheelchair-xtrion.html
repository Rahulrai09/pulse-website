Pulse Compact Indoor-Outdoor Wheelchair: Xtrion

Not every user needs a specialised heavy-duty or ultra-light chair — many just need one balanced wheelchair that handles both home and outdoor settings well. Xtrion is built as that all-rounder.

What is the Pulse Xtrion wheelchair? The Pulse Xtrion is a compact electric wheelchair with a foldable aluminium alloy frame, ergonomic seating, and an intelligent joystick controller for indoor and outdoor use, with a 100 kg load capacity and up to 20 km range per charge.

Who It's For

Xtrion suits users who:

  • Move between indoor and outdoor environments in a typical day, without needing a specialised heavy-duty chair
  • Want a single wheelchair that handles both settings reasonably well, rather than owning two
  • Value ergonomic seating comfort for regular daily use
  • Need dependable mid-range specs — not the lightest chair, not the highest-capacity, but a solid balance

How to Use It

Xtrion is operated via an intelligent joystick controller, with an 8-inch front wheel and 10-inch rear wheel configuration suited to both smooth indoor floors and moderate outdoor surfaces. The foldable aluminium alloy frame allows for storage and transport when not in use, and the ergonomic seating is designed for comfort across extended daily use, up to a 100 kg load capacity and 20 km range.

Why Choose Xtrion Over a Specialised Model

If your daily routine doesn't demand the extremes — you're not navigating rough all-terrain paths daily, and you're not solely focused on minimum transport weight — Xtrion's balanced spec sheet avoids over-paying for capability you won't use, while still handling mixed indoor-outdoor settings competently.
