Circumcision Cost in India: ZSR Stapler, Laser and Open by City

Planning a circumcision for yourself or your child? This guide shows what each method costs in India, the price in big cities, the CGHS rate, what insurance covers and what to ask before you book.

PBy Pulse Team · Updated November 28, 2026 · 5 min read
Share

Circumcision in India usually costs about Rs 18,000 to Rs 40,000 in a private hospital. Yashoda Hospitals lists open surgery at about Rs 18,000, stapler (ZSR) circumcision at about Rs 25,000 and laser circumcision at about Rs 38,000. In big cities, Bajaj Finserv Health lists an average of about Rs 30,500 to Rs 34,150. CGHS pays Rs 6,000 at NABH hospitals for circumcision under local anaesthesia.

Circumcision Cost by Method

There are three common ways to do a circumcision in India. The method changes the price more than anything else. Yashoda Hospitals publishes these approximate prices:

MethodApproximate costHow it works
Open (conventional) circumcisionRs 18,000The surgeon cuts the foreskin with a blade and closes the wound with stitches.
Stapler (ZSR) circumcisionRs 25,000A single-use stapler device cuts and seals the skin in one step.
Laser circumcisionRs 38,000A laser is used to cut the skin, with less bleeding.

These are one hospital chain's figures. Big private hospitals in metro cities often charge more, and small clinics may charge less.

ZSR Circumcision Cost

ZSR is a brand of single-use stapler. Many people say "ZSR" to mean any stapler method. Yashoda Hospitals lists stapler or ZSR circumcision at about Rs 25,000.

In Delhi, Dr Kapil Agrawal of Habilite Clinics puts ZSR circumcision at about Rs 25,000 to Rs 40,000 (December 2025). He says some clinics start from Rs 18,000. The price depends on the hospital, the type of anaesthesia and the quality of the device.

The stapler itself is only a small part of the bill. One stapler costs a few thousand rupees. See our stapler circumcision cost and stapler price guide. The rest pays for the surgeon, the operating room, anaesthesia and follow-up.

Laser Circumcision Cost

Laser circumcision usually costs the most. Yashoda Hospitals lists it at about Rs 38,000. In Delhi, it costs more. Apollo Spectra puts the average at about Rs 51,860 to Rs 59,480 (September 2024).

A higher price does not always mean a better result for every patient. We suggest asking the surgeon which method suits your case, and why.

Adult and Child Circumcision Cost

  • Adults: ZSR and laser surgery are done under local anaesthesia. Habilite Clinics says the patient can go home about an hour later.
  • Children: young children are sometimes put fully to sleep. The fee for this adds to the bill.

Before you compare prices, ask if the quote is for local or general anaesthesia.

CGHS Rate and Government Hospitals

CGHS revised its general surgery rates from 1 February 2024. For this surgery under local anaesthesia, it set:

Hospital typeCGHS rate
NABH-accredited hospitalRs 6,000
Non-NABH hospitalRs 5,100

CGHS changed many rates again in October 2025. Check the current list with your CGHS centre. Government hospitals usually charge far less than private ones, but waiting times can be longer.

Does Health Insurance Cover Circumcision?

Star Health says many health plans cover it when a doctor says it is needed. Common reasons are a tight foreskin (phimosis) or repeated infections. A circumcision done only by choice is usually not covered.

Before surgery, we suggest asking your insurer three things. Is the condition covered? Are day-care procedures included? Is the hospital in the cashless network?

What Is Included in the Price?

  • Surgeon's fee
  • The device, such as a stapler, for stapler circumcision
  • Anaesthesia: local or general
  • Operating room or day-care charges
  • Medicines and dressings
  • Follow-up visits, and taking out staples if needed

Ask for a single package price that lists each item. This makes it easy to compare hospitals fairly.

How to Choose Where to Go

  • See a urologist or general surgeon first. Ask which method suits you, and why.
  • Ask for one written price for the whole package.
  • Check if the price is for local or general anaesthesia.
  • Ask how many of these surgeries the doctor does each month.
  • Ask who to call if there is pain, swelling or bleeding after you go home.
  • Check the follow-up plan, and whether staple removal costs extra.

The cheapest price is not always the best choice. A clear plan and good follow-up matter just as much.
