CBC Test Price in India: Cost by City, CGHS Rate and What It Shows

The complete blood count (CBC) is the most common blood test in India. This guide shows what it costs at large labs and under CGHS, what it measures, and how labs run it.

PBy Pulse Team · Updated November 14, 2026 · 4 min read
Share

A CBC test in India costs about Rs 160 to Rs 480 at large labs, based on published 2026 prices: one national chain lists Rs 160 to Rs 350 by city, and three labs in Bengaluru charge Rs 349 to Rs 480. CGHS pays Rs 300 at NABH-accredited centres in metro cities from 13 October 2025.

What a CBC Test Measures

  • Haemoglobin (Hb) and red cells: red cell count, packed cell volume (PCV) and indices such as MCV, MCH and MCHC. These help find anaemia.
  • White cells: the total count (TLC) and the share of each type (DLC). These help find infection and some blood disorders.
  • Platelets: the cells that help blood clot.

A CBC needs a small blood sample from a vein and no fasting. It shows patterns, not a diagnosis, so a doctor should read the result. Normal ranges vary by lab, age and sex, so compare your result with the range printed on your own report.

Ways to Pay Less

  • Check your health plan: many plans include a yearly check-up, and a CBC is almost always part of it.
  • Consider a package: a basic health package often costs only a little more than a CBC alone.
  • Use your scheme: CGHS and government hospitals charge fixed or low rates.
  • Ask about home collection: most large labs offer it; ask whether there is a fee, as fast-collection services can cost more.

How Labs Run a CBC

Almost every CBC in India is run on an automated haematology analyser. A 3-part analyser sorts white cells into three groups and suits most clinics and small labs; a 5-part analyser gives a fuller white cell breakdown for larger labs and hospitals. CBC Test Price: FAQs

What is the price of a CBC test in India?

About Rs 160 to Rs 480 at large labs, based on published 2026 prices. One national chain lists Rs 160 to Rs 350 by city, and three labs in Bengaluru charged Rs 349 to Rs 480 in July 2026.

What is the CGHS rate for a CBC test?

From 13 October 2025, CGHS pays Rs 300 for a complete haemogram at NABH-accredited centres in metro cities, Rs 270 in Tier Y cities and Rs 240 in Tier Z cities. Centres without accreditation are paid less.

Do I need to fast for a CBC test?

No. A CBC does not need fasting unless your doctor has also ordered other tests that do.

What does a CBC test show?

It measures haemoglobin, red cells, white cells and platelets. It can point to anaemia, infection or a clotting problem, but a doctor needs to read it with your symptoms.

Is home collection available for a CBC?

Yes. Most large labs offer home collection. Ask whether there is a fee, as fast-collection services can cost more.
