An ECG is one of the cheapest and most common heart tests. This guide shows what it costs in Indian cities, what CGHS pays, and how it compares with a TMT or Holter test.

What an ECG Shows

An ECG (electrocardiogram) records the electrical signal of the heart through small sticky pads on the chest, arms and legs. A standard resting ECG uses 12 leads, which give 12 views of the heart. It can show:

  • an irregular or very fast or slow heartbeat;
  • signs of a past or current heart attack, or reduced blood flow;
  • a thick heart wall or other strain on the heart;
  • the effect of some medicines and salts such as potassium.

It is quick and painless and needs no fasting. A doctor should read the result.

ECG vs TMT vs Holter

  • Resting ECG: a few minutes at rest. The cheapest and most common test.
  • TMT (stress test): an ECG while you walk on a treadmill, to check how the heart copes with effort.
  • Holter: a small recorder worn for 24 hours or more, to catch beats that come and go.

ECG Test Price: FAQs

What is the price of an ECG test in India?

About Rs 150 to Rs 900, depending on the city and the centre, according to a 2025 city price list from Apollo 24|7.

What is the CGHS rate for an ECG?

From 13 October 2025, CGHS pays Rs 175 for an ECG at NABH-accredited centres in metro cities, Rs 158 in Tier Y cities and Rs 140 in Tier Z cities.

Can I get an ECG at home?

Yes, in many cities. Some diagnostic chains send a technician home to do the ECG, so ask whether a home visit costs extra.

What is the difference between an ECG, TMT and Holter test?

A resting ECG takes a few minutes at rest. A TMT records the ECG while you walk on a treadmill. A Holter records it for 24 hours or more while you go about your day.

Do I need to fast before an ECG?

No. An ECG needs no fasting. Wear clothes that let the pads be placed on your chest, arms and legs.
