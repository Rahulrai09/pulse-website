FAQ

Request a Quote FAQs

Still have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How do I get a quote for medical equipment from Pulse?

Fill in the quote form on this page or send the details on WhatsApp to +91 90711 01108. Share the product, quantity, your city and the type of facility, and the team will send a written quote.

What should I include in a quote request?

Include the product or model, the quantity, your city, the type of facility, must-have features and your timeline. If you are buying through a tender, add the tender details.

Can I get one quote for several products?

Yes. Choose "Several products / full list" and list the items in the notes, or send the list on WhatsApp. Pulse can quote a single machine, a full department or a complete hospital setup.

Can Pulse quote products that are not on the website?

Yes. Pulse is not limited to the products listed on its website. Choose "Something not listed" and describe what you need.

Does Pulse supply outside Bengaluru?

Yes. Pulse supplies and services hospitals, clinics and labs across India. Add your city so the team can plan delivery and installation.

Can I see a demo before I buy?

Yes. Ask for a demo in your quote request and the team will arrange one where possible.

Where can I find typical prices before asking for a quote?

Pulse publishes price guides for common equipment, such as ICU ventilators, ICU beds, patient monitors, ECG machines and dialysis machines. See the medical equipment price list on the Pulse blog.
