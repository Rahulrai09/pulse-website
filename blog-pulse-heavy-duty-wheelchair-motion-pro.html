Pulse Heavy-Duty All-Terrain Wheelchair: Motion Pro-1 & Motion Pro-2

Not every power wheelchair needs to be as light as possible. For users who move across outdoor paths, kerbs and uneven ground every day, strength and stability matter more than saving a few kilograms. That is what the Motion Pro range is built for.

What is the Pulse Motion Pro wheelchair? The Pulse Motion Pro-1 and Motion Pro-2 are heavy-duty folding power wheelchairs with a steel frame that carries up to 140 kg. Both run on a 24V 20Ah lithium-ion battery for about 15 km per charge. Motion Pro-2 adds large 23-inch rear wheels that can also be pushed by hand.

Who Needs the Motion Pro

Motion Pro suits users who:

  • Move across outdoor paths, gravel, kerbs or uneven ground often
  • Weigh up to 140 kg
  • Use a wheelchair all day, every day
  • Want a strong, stable chair more than the lightest one

If frequent air travel or lifting the chair into a car is the priority, compare Pulse's lighter Aerodrive or Smartride models instead.

Operating the Motion Pro

Both models use joystick control, two 250 W motors (500 W in total) and electromagnetic brakes. They fold with an X-fold design, but being heavy-duty, they are bulkier than Pulse's light models. The roomy 44 x 44 cm seat suits long hours of sitting.

Motion Pro-1 or Motion Pro-2?

Motion Pro-1 has 10-inch front and 16-inch rear wheels and weighs 36 kg without the battery. Motion Pro-2 weighs 31 kg and has large 23-inch rear wheels, so a carer or the user can also push it by hand if the battery runs low.

Flying with the Motion Pro

Airlines care about how much energy a wheelchair battery stores, measured in watt-hours (Wh). You work it out as volts × amp-hours. The Motion Pro uses a 24V 20Ah lithium-ion battery, which is about 480 Wh. That is above the 300 Wh limit that airline safety rules set for a battery removed from a wheelchair. If the battery stays fitted in the chair there is no watt-hour limit, but you must get the airline's approval in advance.

Always tell your airline before you fly. Removed and spare batteries must go in the cabin with their terminals covered. You may carry one spare battery up to 300 Wh, or two spares up to 160 Wh each.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who should choose a heavy-duty wheelchair like Motion Pro?

Users who need daily mobility across outdoor paths, kerbs and uneven ground, and users up to 140 kg who want a strong, stable chair more than the lightest one.

What's the difference between Motion Pro-1 and Motion Pro-2?

Both have a steel frame, 140 kg capacity, 24V 20Ah battery and about 15 km range. Motion Pro-1 weighs 36 kg without the battery and has 16-inch rear wheels. Motion Pro-2 weighs 31 kg and has 23-inch rear wheels that can be pushed by hand.

How do you fold and transport a Motion Pro wheelchair?

Both fold with an X-fold design, but they are bulkier than Pulse's light models. Motion Pro-1 folds to about 82 x 46 x 70 cm, so check your car boot size before you buy.
