Laser Hair Removal Cost in India: Price per Session by Body Area

Laser hair removal is one of the most asked-about skin treatments in India. This guide shows what a session costs by body area, how many sessions most people need, and what clinics consider when they price it.

Laser Hair Removal Cost per Session

These per-session ranges come from a health insurer's 2026 guide (Star Health). Prices vary by city, clinic and machine.

Body areaPrice per session
Face (upper lip, chin, sideburns)Rs 1,500 – 3,000
UnderarmsRs 2,000 – 4,000
Bikini lineRs 2,500 – 5,000
Half legs (upper or lower)Rs 4,000 – 7,000
Full legsRs 7,000 – 12,000
Chest or back (men)Rs 6,000 – 10,000
Full bodyRs 15,000 – 25,000

As a cross-check, one national skin clinic chain lists indicative per-session prices of Rs 2,380 to Rs 3,400 for the upper lip or underarms, and Rs 6,440 to Rs 9,200 for the full face, full arms, full legs or full back.

How Many Sessions, and the Total Cost

Laser works only on hairs in the growing stage, so it takes several sessions. The insurer guide says most people need 6 to 8; the clinic chain says 4 to 6 is common, with maintenance sessions later. Hormonal conditions such as PCOS can mean more sessions.

Sessions are usually spaced 4 to 8 weeks apart, so a course takes several months. The insurer guide puts a full course at about Rs 15,000 to Rs 80,000. Packages for several areas often cost less than paying per session.

What Changes the Price

  • Area size: larger areas take more time and more laser pulses.
  • Hair and skin type: thick, dark hair responds well; fine or light hair needs more sessions.
  • Machine: clinics with newer diode or multi-wavelength lasers may charge more.
  • City and clinic: metro and dermatologist-led clinics sit at the top of the range.

Is Laser Hair Removal Safe for Indian Skin?

Yes, when it is done by trained staff with the right machine and settings. Longer wavelengths such as 808 nm and 1064 nm are generally safer for darker skin. See our laser hair removal machine for Indian skin guide for how the laser types compare.

Does Insurance Cover Laser Hair Removal?

Usually not. Most health plans treat it as a cosmetic procedure and exclude it. Ask the clinic about packages or monthly payment plans instead.

For Clinics: What a Session Has to Pay For

Entry-level diode laser machines cost about Rs 8 lakh to Rs 15 lakh in India, and medical-grade systems about Rs 20 lakh to Rs 40 lakh (see our laser hair removal machine price guide). As a rough example (our estimate), a Rs 15 lakh machine needs about 500 underarm sessions at Rs 3,000 just to cover the machine, before rent, staff, power and handpiece costs.
